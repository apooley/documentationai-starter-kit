title: Voice Conversations
description: Review phone calls handled by your AI assistant. Search calls, listen to recordings, read summaries, assign and tag calls, and export or clean up in bulk.
---

When customers call your business number and an AI assistant answers, each call appears on the **Phone / Voice** page. You can see who called, how long it lasted, a short summary, and listen to the recording.

Open **Conversations → Phone / Voice** in the sidebar.

<Callout kind="info">
Phone / Voice only appears if your plan includes the voice add-on and phone numbers are set up for your account. If the add-on isn't on your plan, the page says "Voice Not Available."
</Callout>

## Set up voice calls

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get a phone number">
    Request a number in **Settings → Phone Numbers**. See [Phone Numbers](/pages/settings/phone-numbers).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose and tune an assistant">
    In **AI Assistant → Assistants**, open (**Edit**) the assistant that should answer calls. Choose a **Voice** for it, and set it as **Default for Voice** if it should answer every call. See [AI Assistants](/pages/ai-config/assistants).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add routing rules (optional)">
    If you have several assistants, create [routing rules](/pages/ai-config/routing-handoffs) and tick **voice** under channels.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The call list

The page title shows how many calls you have in total. Each row shows:

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Status** | **Completed**, **Active** (in progress), **Missed** (the call closed without a normal end), or **Ended**. |
| **Phone Number** | The caller's number. |
| **Contact** | Name (or Unknown), company, an unread badge, tags, and who it's **Assigned** to. |
| **Duration** | Call length as minutes:seconds. |
| **Summary** | An AI summary of the call: key points, outcome and mood, plus who spoke last and when. |
| **Recording** | **Listen** opens the recording in a new tab, or "No recording." |
| **Lifecycle** | Open, Pending, Snoozed (with an "Until" date) or Resolved. |
| **Actions** | A **Status** drop-down and an **Assign...** drop-down. |
| **Started** | When the call began. |

Click a row to open the full call, with transcript, recording player, notes and contact details. See [Conversation Detail](/pages/conversations/conversation-detail).

## Find calls

- **Search by phone number or transcript...** finds calls by number or by words said in the call.
- **All / Completed / Active / Missed** buttons filter by call status.
- **All assignees** drop-down: choose Unassigned or a team member. **My Conversations** shows only your calls.
- **All lifecycle statuses** drop-down: Open, Pending, Snoozed or Resolved.
- **Tags**: if you've set up conversation tags, click a tag to filter. Create tags in [Conversation Tags](/pages/settings/conversation-tags).

If nothing matches, click **Clear Filters**. Click **Load More** at the bottom for older calls.

## Review a call

1. Click the call row.
2. Read the **Agent Summary** and **Transcript**.
3. In the **Call Recording** panel, press play. It shows the duration and offers a download link when a recording is available.

Transcripts show what the AI said and how the caller replied, so they are the quickest way to find where a call went off track. Use your browser's find (Ctrl+F, or Cmd+F on a Mac) to search for a word.

## After the call

On the call's page you can:

- Add private **Notes** for your team.
- Add **Tags** to categorise the call.
- Use **Link to Client Record** if it wasn't matched to a client automatically.
- Set the status to **Resolved** when you've dealt with it.
- Choose an owner in **Assign...**.

## Work on many calls at once

Tick calls using the checkboxes. A bar appears with:

- **Assign selected...** then **Assign**
- **Set status...** then **Update Status**
- **Export Selected** to download the selected calls as a CSV file
- **Clear Selection**
- **Delete N** to permanently delete them (you'll be asked to confirm)

<Callout kind="alert">
Deleting conversations cannot be undone.
</Callout>
