Click **View** on any row in [Conversations](/pages/conversations/conversations) to open **Conversation Details**. This is where you read the whole conversation, reply (on chat and text channels), and see what the AI learned about the customer.

Use **Back to conversations** at the top left to return to the list.

## The header

At the top you'll see the contact's name, their channel and company, and their tags. On the right:

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Status** | Open, Pending, Snoozed (24h) or Resolved. |
| **Assign...** | Choose a team member, or **Unassigned**. |
| **Tags** | Add or remove tags. Create tags in [Conversation Tags](/pages/settings/conversation-tags). |
| Status badge | Shows the current status. A **Training: Good** or **Training: Bad** badge appears if the conversation was flagged for training review. |

<Callout kind="info">
**Snoozed (24h)** sets the conversation aside for 24 hours. Choose **Open** or **Resolved** to change it sooner.
</Callout>

## The transcript

The **Transcript** card shows every message in order with its time, plus attachments. For text channels each message you send shows **Sent**, **Delivered** or **Read** where available. Use the load-earlier option at the top to see older messages.

### Agent Summary

When available, a purple **Agent Summary** card sits at the top of the transcript. It can include:

- **Conversation Summary**: a short AI summary.
- **Business Context**: industry, company size, use case, pain points, decision criteria.
- **Sentiment**: overall mood, urgency and engagement.
- **Decision Making**: the contact's role, authority and buying stage.
- **Qualification** and **Notes**.

A summary is created when a conversation ends, including when it closes automatically after being quiet. A small **Inactive** badge marks conversations closed that way.

## Reply to a customer

You can reply directly on **chat, SMS, Messenger and Instagram** conversations. Email and voice conversations are view-only here.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Type in the message box">
    Click the box that says "Message *customer name*..." at the bottom of the transcript.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use a canned response (optional)">
    Type `/` and the start of a shortcut. A list of matching canned responses appears. Use the arrow keys and press Enter or Tab (or click one) to insert it, then edit if you like.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send">
    Press **Enter**, or click the blue send arrow. Press **Shift + Enter** for a new line.
  </Step>
</Steps>

For SMS, a counter shows characters used out of 160. Longer messages may be split into several texts.

<Callout kind="tip">
Canned responses are saved replies your team sets up once and reuses.
</Callout>

## Review an AI chat

On **chat** conversations, a **Review** button appears next to the transcript title (when AI Playbooks is turned on for your account). It opens **Training review**, where an AI interviewer debriefs the chat with you.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Answer the interviewer">
    The AI asks about the conversation. Type your answers in the box ("Share what was good or bad about this conversation…") and press Enter.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the findings">
    When the interview finishes you'll see a **Review summary** (with an overall sentiment), the individual findings, **Proposed playbook changes**, **Proposed assistant changes** and **Additional guidance**. **Open playbook** and **Open assistant** links take you to the thing that would change.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply, or close">
    Click **Apply playbook changes** (optionally ticking **Activate new playbook version immediately**) or **Apply assistant changes**. Or click **Close** to leave without changing anything.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="alert">
Playbook changes affect **every** assistant that uses that playbook. Assistant changes affect only this assistant.
</Callout>

See [Training Review](/pages/ai-config/training-review), [AI Playbooks](/pages/ai-config/playbooks) and [AI Assistants](/pages/ai-config/assistants).

## The right-hand side

### Contact Information

Shows the name, company, phone and email collected so far, or "No contact information collected yet." Use **Link to Client Record** to connect the conversation to a client in your CRM.

If your account has a CRM connected, Autoch.at suggests a matching client in a **Client Linking** banner. You can accept or dismiss it. If it linked one automatically, you can view the client or **Unlink**.

### Qualification Data

Fields the AI collected, such as **Budget**, **Timeline**, **Website Count** and **Current Provider**.

### Notes

A private notes box for your team. The customer never sees it. It saves automatically as you type, and **Note History** lists earlier notes.

### Call Recording (voice)

On phone conversations, a **Call Recording** panel lets you play the recording when one is available. See [Voice](/pages/conversations/voice).

### Email threads and workflows (email)

- A blue bar shows "Thread 2 of 3" and lets you jump to the **Previous in thread** or **Next in thread** email.
- A card shows which [email workflow](/pages/conversations/email-workflows) handled the email and why: the keywords found, or "Fallback match (no specific keywords matched)". **Test Workflow Matching** lets you enter an **Email Subject** and **Email Body** to see which workflow would match.

### Chat Widget Tracking (chat)

If you use widget tracking, this card lists the tracking events sent for this chat, with filters **All, Browser, Webhook, Failed**.

## Delivery & Sync

Below the transcript, **Delivery & Sync** keeps a history of what was sent out about this conversation: CRM syncs, lead emails (including ADF), email and SMS notifications and webhooks. Each row shows **When**, **Type**, **Status** and **Detail**. Failed items show in red with the error.

A **Delivery status** card on the right gives the latest result at a glance.

### Resend an ADF lead email

If an ADF (structured XML lead email) failed or was missed:

1. Open the conversation.
2. In the **Delivery status** card, click **Resend ADF**.
3. A message confirms how many recipients it was sent to.

The email is rebuilt from the conversation's current contact and summary details. Nothing is re-summarised. ADF lead emails are set up in **Settings → Notifications**.
