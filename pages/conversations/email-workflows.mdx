**Email Workflows** tell your AI how to handle incoming email. Each workflow matches certain emails (for example, sales questions or support requests), gives the AI the right background information, and optionally runs steps such as pulling out the sender's details.

Open **Conversations → Email Workflows** in the sidebar.

<Callout kind="info">
Email Workflows needs the email channel on your plan and the feature turned on for your account. If it isn't in your sidebar, ask your administrator or support.
</Callout>

## How a workflow works

1. An email arrives at your Autoch.at email address.
2. Autoch.at checks your **active** workflows, highest **Priority** first, and picks the first one that matches.
3. The assigned assistant (or your default email assistant) replies, using the workflow's **Context for AI Agent** as guidance, and runs the workflow's steps.

The workflow's **Goal** decides how it matches:

| Goal | How it matches |
|---|---|
| **General Inquiry** | Every email. Use it as your catch-all and give it a low priority so more specific workflows are checked first. |
| **Lead Capture** | The subject or body contains a sales word such as pricing, quote, interested, budget, cost, price or buy. |
| **Help Desk Support** | The subject or body contains a support word such as support, help, issue, problem, error, bug, broken or "not working". |
| **Appointment Booking** and **Sales Inquiry** | No automatic keyword matching. They are only used when no other workflow matches, in which case your highest-priority workflow is used. |

## Check your email address

Click **Email Settings** at the top to see:

- **Your Incoming Email Address**, with a **Copy** button. Emails sent to it are handled by your workflows.
- **Email Verified** or **Not Verified**, and **Auto-Respond Enabled** if automatic replies are on.
- A link to **Settings → Email** for sending setup. See [Email settings](/pages/settings/email).

## Create a workflow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click New Workflow">
    Choose **Start from scratch** or pick a template: **Lead Capture**, **Help Desk Support**, **Appointment Booking**, **General Inquiry** or **Sales Inquiry**. Each template shows its goal and how many steps it has.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it and choose a Goal">
    Fill in **Workflow Name** (required), **Goal** (required) and an optional **Description**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add context for the AI">
    In **Context for AI Agent**, describe your business, services, pricing and policies. The AI uses this as guidance and adapts it naturally. It isn't copied word for word.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose who answers (optional)">
    **Assigned Agent (Optional)** picks a specific assistant. Leave it as "Use default agent or routing rules" to use your usual email assistant.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Priority">
    A higher number is checked first. If an email could match several workflows, the one with the highest priority wins.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add steps (optional)">
    Under **Workflow Steps**, click **Add Step** (see below). Switch between **List View** and **Visual Editor** to see the steps as a list or as a diagram.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save Workflow**. New workflows start **Active**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Steps you can add

Steps run in order from top to bottom. Use the up and down arrows to reorder, **Configure** to open the settings, and the bin to remove one. Each step also has an optional **Condition (optional)** box, for example `email contains 'urgent'`, so it only runs when that is true.

| Step | What it does | Settings |
|---|---|---|
| **Extract Information** | Looks for details in the email text, such as name, email and phone. | **Fields to Extract (comma-separated)**, for example `name, email, company, phone`. |
| **Send Email Response** | The AI writes a reply using your context. It is queued with a short delay and includes the assistant's signature. | **Response Template**: used only if the AI can't write one. Insert extracted details with `{name}`, `{email}` and so on. |
| **Create Support Ticket** | Creates a ticket with a reference number. | **Ticket System** (Internal System, Zendesk, Freshdesk, Jira) and **Priority** (Low, Medium, High, Urgent). |
| **Book Appointment** | Adds a booking link to your reply. | **Calendar System** (Calendly, Cal.com, Google Calendar) and **Booking Link**. |
| **Save to Database** | Saves the extracted details. | **Table/Collection Name**, for example `leads`. |

<Callout kind="tip">
Start simple: one workflow with a good **Context for AI Agent** and a single **Send Email Response** step. Test it, then add more.
</Callout>

## Test which workflow will match

Once you have workflows, a **Test Workflow Matching** box appears on the page:

1. Type an **Email Subject**, for example "Pricing inquiry".
2. Type an **Email Body**, for example "I'm interested in your pricing plans…".
3. Click **Test Match**.

You see the **Matched Workflow** (with its priority) and why, plus **All Workflows Checked** with a MATCHED label on the winner. Testing does not send anything.

## Manage workflows

Each workflow card shows its name, **Active** or **Inactive**, its goal, number of **Steps** and **Priority**. Use:

- **Deactivate / Activate** to pause one without deleting it.
- The pencil icon to edit.
- The bin icon to delete it (you'll be asked to confirm).

## Context Library

Click **Context Library** to store reusable snippets, such as `company_info`, that you want available across workflows. Enter a **Context key** and **Context value** and click **Add**. Use the bin to remove one.

## See which workflow handled an email

Open any email in [Conversation Detail](/pages/conversations/conversation-detail). A card shows the workflow that matched, its priority and the keywords that triggered it.
