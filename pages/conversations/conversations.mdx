The **Conversations** page lists every customer conversation, across all channels, in one place. From here you can find a conversation, see who is handling it, update its status, and open it to read or reply.

Open it from **Conversations** in the left sidebar.

## Where to find each channel

Under **Conversations** in the sidebar you'll see:

| Sidebar item | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Chat Conversations** | The list filtered to website chat. |
| **SMS Conversations** | Text messages (if SMS is on your plan). |
| **Email Conversations** | Email threads (if email is on your plan). |
| **Phone / Voice** | Phone calls (if voice is set up). |
| **Widget Playground** | A safe place to test your assistant. See [Widget Playground](/pages/conversations/widget-playground). |

You may also see **Email Workflows**, **Outbound Email** and **SMS Workflows** if they're turned on for your account.

## Read the list

Each row shows:

- **Channel**: chat, SMS, email, voice, messenger or instagram. A green or red flag next to it means the conversation was flagged good or bad for training review.
- **Contact**: name (or Unknown), phone or email, an **unread** badge, any tags, who it's **Assigned** to, the latest message, and how long ago.
- **AI Context** (on wider screens): quick badges such as Critical or High Urgency, Frustrated, Negative or Positive mood, and Decision Stage or Considering.
- **Status**: Open, Pending, Snoozed (with "Until" date) or Resolved.
- **Qualification** (on very wide screens): Qualified, Unqualified or Pending, with a score and short reason when available.
- **Actions**: **View**, and a **⋯** menu.

Unread conversations are lightly highlighted. If the list is long, click **Load More** at the bottom.

## Search and filter

- **Search conversations...** looks at contact name, phone number, email and company. Matches are highlighted.
- **Channel buttons:** All, Voice, SMS, Chat, Email, Messenger and Instagram, each with a count. **My Conversations** shows only ones assigned to you.
- **Lifecycle status** drop-down: All lifecycle statuses, Open, Pending, Snoozed, Resolved.
- **Filters** button opens more options:
  - **Date From** / **Date To**
  - **Qualification Status**: All, Qualified or Unqualified
  - **Training review**: All, Good or Bad (see [Training Review](/pages/ai-config/training-review))
  - **Assignee**: All assignees, Unassigned, or a team member
  - **Tags**: click tags to filter. Create tags in [Conversation Tags](/pages/settings/conversation-tags).

Use filters together, for example: Email + Open + Unassigned. Click **Clear All** to reset.

<Callout kind="info">
Refreshing the page keeps you where you were. If you opened Chat from the sidebar, the list stays on chat.
</Callout>

## Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Open** | Active and needs attention. |
| **Pending** | Waiting for the customer or for something else. |
| **Snoozed** | Set aside for 24 hours. The row shows when the snooze ends. |
| **Resolved** | Finished. |

Conversations that go quiet are closed automatically, by default after about 30 minutes without a message. Closing also creates an AI summary for the conversation.

## Change one conversation

Click **⋯** (Conversation actions) on a row:

- **View details** opens the conversation. See [Conversation Detail](/pages/conversations/conversation-detail).
- **Set lifecycle status** → Open, Pending, Snoozed (24h) or Resolved.
- **Assign conversation** → Unassigned or a team member.
- **Delete conversation**: permanent.

## Change many at once

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tick the conversations">
    Use the checkbox on each row, or the top checkbox for all rows on screen. A blue bar appears showing "N conversations selected".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose an action">
    - **Assign selected...** then **Assign**
    - **Set status...** then **Update Status**
    - **Export Selected** downloads them as a CSV file
    - **Delete N** permanently deletes them (you're asked to confirm)
    - **Clear Selection** un-ticks everything
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="alert">
Deleting conversations cannot be undone. Export first if you might need a record.
</Callout>

## Export

Click **Export** at the top to download a CSV file of the conversations that match your channel, date and qualification filters.

## Keyboard shortcuts

Turn them on in **Settings → Preferences** with **Enable Shortcuts** (they're off by default). Then press **g** followed by a letter: **g d** Dashboard, **g c** Conversations, **g a** Analytics, **g s** Settings. Press **?** to see the full list.
