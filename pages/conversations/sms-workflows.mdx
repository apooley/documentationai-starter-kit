**SMS Workflows** let you guide your AI on text-message conversations: what the goal is, what background information it should use, and which assistant should handle it.

Open **Conversations → SMS Workflows** in the sidebar.

<Callout kind="info">
SMS Workflows needs the SMS channel on your plan, and the feature must be turned on for your account. If it isn't in your sidebar, ask your administrator or support. See also [Phone Numbers](/pages/settings/phone-numbers) to get a number for texting.
</Callout>

## The SMS Workflows page

Each workflow appears as a card showing its name, an **Active** badge when it's switched on, its goal, a short description, and how many steps it has and its **Priority**. Use the pencil icon to edit and the bin icon to delete (you'll be asked to confirm).

If you have none yet you'll see "No SMS workflows yet" with a **Create Workflow** button.

## Create a workflow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click New Workflow">
    The editor opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it and pick a Goal">
    Enter a **Workflow Name** (for example "Lead Capture") and choose a **Goal**:

    - **General Inquiry**: the catch-all. It matches every message.
    - **Lead Capture**: for people showing buying interest.
    - **Appointment Booking**
    - **Help Desk Support**: for people asking for help.
    - **Sales Inquiry**

    A **Description** is optional but helps your team remember what it's for.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add context for the AI">
    In **Context for AI Agent**, describe your business, services, pricing and policies. The AI uses this as guidance and adapts it naturally. It is not a script it fills in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a Response Template (optional)">
    A sample reply the AI can adapt. Keep it short; texts are limited to 160 characters per segment, and longer ones are split into several.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose an assistant (optional)">
    **Assigned Agent (Optional)** picks a specific assistant. Leave it on "Use default agent or routing rules" to use your usual SMS assistant. See [AI Assistants](/pages/ai-config/assistants) and [Routing & Handoffs](/pages/ai-config/routing-handoffs).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Priority">
    A higher number is checked first. Give specific workflows a higher priority than your General Inquiry catch-all.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add steps (optional)">
    Under **Workflow Steps**, click **Add Step** to describe what should happen. Each step has settings you open with **Configure**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch on Active and save">
    Tick **Active**, then save. Inactive workflows are kept but not used.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">
Start with one workflow and a clear **Context for AI Agent**. Add more only when you see a need.
</Callout>

## Tips for text messages

- **Keep it short.** Each text segment is 160 characters. Longer replies are split into several messages.
- **Be thoughtful about timing.** Avoid contacting people in the middle of the night, and follow your local rules on consent for marketing messages. Getting consent is your responsibility.
- Customers can ask for a person by replying with words like "human" or "agent". See [Routing & Handoffs](/pages/ai-config/routing-handoffs).
