A **profile** is a named, coloured folder for your social accounts. Use one profile per brand, location or client so posts, queues and analytics stay organised. Connecting the actual Facebook, Instagram or other accounts happens on the [Social Accounts](/pages/social/social-accounts) page.

Find it in the left menu under **Social Media** > **Profiles**.

<Callout kind="info">
Social Media is an optional add-on and is switched on **per workspace**. If the **Social Media** section is missing from your menu, the add-on isn't active for your account. Contact support or your account owner.
</Callout>

## Before you start: turn social on for your workspace

On the Profiles page, the **Workspace** box shows the workspace you're working in (switch workspaces from the sidebar). Next to the name you'll see "(Social enabled)" when it's on.

If you see "Social not enabled for this workspace.", click **Enable social add-on** to go to **Settings** > **Workspaces**. See [Workspaces](/pages/settings/workspaces).

## Create your first profile

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start">
    If this is your first time you'll see **Set Up Your Social Media Profile**. Click **Set Up Profile**. Otherwise click **Create Profile**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the form">
    Enter a **Name** (required, up to 255 characters). Optionally add a **Description** and pick a **Color**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Create**. You'll see "Profile created successfully".
  </Step>
</Steps>

After your first profile exists, **Posts**, **Queues** and **Analytics** appear in the **Social Media** menu.

## Manage profiles

Each profile appears as a card with its colour, name and description.

- Click the **pencil** icon to rename it or change its description or colour, then click **Update**.
- Click **View Accounts** to see the accounts inside that profile.
- Click the **bin** icon to delete it.

<Callout kind="alert">
Deleting a profile also deletes all of its connected accounts, posts and queues. You'll be asked to confirm, and it can't be undone.
</Callout>

## What's next

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect accounts">
    Go to [Social Accounts](/pages/social/social-accounts) and connect the pages and profiles you want to post to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write and schedule posts">
    Use [Posts](/pages/social/social-posts), or let [Caption Generator](/pages/social/caption-generator) draft your text.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a posting rhythm">
    Add weekly time slots with [Queues](/pages/social/social-queues).
  </Step>
</Steps>
