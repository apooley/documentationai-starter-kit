**Caption Generator** writes social media captions for you. Tell it what the post is about and it drafts several options in your brand's voice, using the information you've already given Autoch.at.

Find it in the left menu under **Social Media** > **Caption Generator**.

## Generate captions

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick the platform">
    Under **Platform**, choose where the post will go: Twitter, Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, TikTok, YouTube, Pinterest, Reddit, Bluesky, Threads or Google Business. Each has its own style and length limits.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Say what it's about">
    In **Topic / Prompt**, describe the post. The more detail, the better. For example: "Announcing our spring cleaning special: 20% off until Friday, for homeowners in Ottawa."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what the AI can draw on">
    Under **Context Sources**, tick the information to use: **Branding**, **Products**, **Documents**, **Website** and **Instructions**. **Branding** is ticked to begin with. You need at least one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set tone and length (optional)">
    Choose a **Tone**: Professional, Casual, Friendly, Humorous, Inspirational or Educational. Choose a **Length**: Short, Medium or Long. Leave them on **Default** to let the AI decide.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate">
    Click **Generate Captions**. After a moment you'll see **Generated Captions** with several numbered variations.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Use a caption

Each variation shows the caption plus suggested **Emojis**, **Hashtags** and **Mentions**.

1. Click **Copy**. It turns to "Copied!" and puts the caption, emojis, hashtags and mentions on your clipboard together.
2. Go to [Posts](/pages/social/social-posts), click **Create Post**, and paste it into **Content**.
3. Edit it, choose your accounts, add media and schedule or queue it.

Want a different set? Change your topic or settings and click **Generate Captions** again. There's no separate "regenerate" button.

## Get better results

- **Be specific.** "Announcing our new AI-powered scheduling tool, launching next Tuesday, for small business owners" beats "write about our product".
- **Pick the right platform first.** Twitter needs to be short, Instagram can be longer with hashtags, LinkedIn suits a professional tone.
- **Add facts the AI can't know**, such as exact dates, prices or promo codes. Always check them before posting.
- **Fill in your Training Context.** Branding, products, documents and your website feed the generator. See [Training Context](/pages/ai-config/training-context).

<Callout kind="alert">
Treat generated captions as a first draft. Read them before you publish, and check that names, dates, prices and claims are correct.
</Callout>

## If something goes wrong

- **"Please enter a topic or prompt"**: type something in **Topic / Prompt**.
- **"Please select at least one context source"**: tick at least one box.
- **"Failed to generate captions. Please try again."**: wait a moment and click **Generate Captions** again.
