**Social Posts** is where you write, schedule and track your social media posts across every connected account.

Find it in the left menu under **Social Media** > **Posts**. The **Posts** item appears once you've created a [profile](/pages/social/social-profiles) and connected at least one [account](/pages/social/social-accounts).

## Create a post

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the composer">
    Click **Create Post** (top right).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a profile">
    Pick the **Profile** the post belongs to. The accounts you can post to come from that profile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write your text">
    Type in **Content**. A counter under the box shows "0 / limit characters". The limit is the lowest among the platforms you tick, so you can't accidentally write too much for one of them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose where it goes">
    Tick the connected accounts to publish to. You can pick several at once. You must choose at least one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add media (optional)">
    Use **Media** to upload up to 10 images or videos.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Timing">
    Under **Schedule for (optional)** pick a date and time and your **Timezone** (UTC, Eastern, Central, Mountain or Pacific). Leave it empty to save the post as a draft.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Create Post**. If you left the schedule empty you can instead click **Create & Add to Queue** to send it straight to the profile's [queue](/pages/social/social-queues).
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Character limits

| Platform | Limit |
|---|---|
| X / Twitter | 280 |
| Bluesky | 300 |
| Pinterest, Threads | 500 |
| Google Business | 1,500 |
| Instagram, TikTok | 2,200 |
| LinkedIn | 3,000 |
| Facebook, YouTube | 5,000 |
| Reddit | 10,000 |

<Callout kind="tip">
Stuck for words? Open [Caption Generator](/pages/social/caption-generator), copy the result, and paste it into **Content**.
</Callout>

## Find your posts

Each post card shows a status badge, the scheduled or published date, the text, the platforms and any media thumbnails.

- **Search posts...** searches the text of your posts.
- The status dropdown filters by **Draft**, **Scheduled**, **Published** or **Failed**.
- **All Profiles** lets you narrow to one profile.

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Draft** | Saved but not scheduled or published. |
| **Scheduled** | Set to publish at a specific date and time. |
| **Published** | Posted to the platform. |
| **Failed** | Something went wrong when publishing. |

## Actions on a post

The icons at the right of each post:

- **Chart icon (View analytics)** opens [Social Analytics](/pages/social/social-analytics) for that post. The **Analytics** button at the top opens the overall view.
- **Clock icon (Add to queue)** appears on drafts. It places the post in the next free slot in the profile's queue.
- **Retry icon** appears on failed posts. Click it to try publishing again.
- **Pencil (Edit post)** reopens the composer. Change what you need and click **Update Post**.
- **Bin (Delete post)** removes it after you confirm.

When you filter by **Failed**, a **Retry all** button appears to retry every failed post at once.

<Callout kind="info">
Once a post has been published on a social network, changing or deleting it here may not change what people already see there. If you need to correct a published post, check it on the network itself too.
</Callout>

## Troubleshooting

- **"Please select at least one platform"**: tick at least one connected account in the composer. If none are listed, connect one under [Social Accounts](/pages/social/social-accounts).
- **A post shows Failed**: click the retry icon. If it keeps failing, reconnect the account in Social Accounts and try again.
- **"Post must have a profile to assign to queue"**: edit the post and choose a profile first.
