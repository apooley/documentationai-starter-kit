**Social Accounts** is where you connect the social media accounts your business uses. Accounts are grouped under the [profiles](/pages/social/social-profiles) you created.

Find it in the left menu under **Social Media** > **Accounts**.

<Callout kind="info">
You must have a workspace with the social add-on switched on. The **Workspace** box at the top shows the one you're working in. If it says "Social add-on is not enabled for this workspace.", click **Enable social add-on**.
</Callout>

## Two ways to connect

Click **Connect Account** (top right). A window titled **Connect Social Account** offers two options:

| Option | Use it for | Platforms |
|---|---|---|
| **GetLate Connector** | **Posting and scheduling** only. It does *not* connect Facebook Messenger or Instagram DMs. | Twitter, Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, TikTok, YouTube, Pinterest, Reddit, Bluesky, Threads, Google Business |
| **Native Meta Connector** | **Messenger and Instagram DMs** appearing in your Conversations inbox, for inbound messaging and not just posts. | Facebook and Instagram |

## Connect an account for posting

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick a profile">
    Under **GetLate Connector**, choose a **Profile**. If the list is empty, create one first: "Create a social profile first to connect via GetLate."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the platform">
    Choose the **Platform**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect">
    Click **Connect via GetLate**. A sign-in window opens for that platform. If nothing appears, allow pop-ups for this site ("Popup blocked. Please allow popups for this site.").
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve">
    Sign in with the account that manages your business page and approve access. Click **Refresh** if the new account doesn't show up straight away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Use an account that manages the business page or profile, not a personal one you don't control.

## Connect Facebook and Instagram for DMs

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the Native Meta Connector">
    In **Connect Social Account**, find **Native Meta Connector**. Check the workspace shown, then choose a **Starting platform** (Facebook or Instagram).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect">
    Click **Connect via Native Meta** and sign in to Meta.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Link your assets">
    When you return, the **Link Meta Assets** window lists your Facebook pages. Tick each page to connect. To also use its Instagram business account, tick **Enable linked Instagram business account**. That box is greyed out if the page has no Instagram business account linked.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finish">
    Click **Link Selected Assets**. The pages appear under **Native Meta Accounts**, "Connected directly via Meta OAuth".
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="alert">
If you see "Native Meta connect is currently disabled for this tenant.", this connector isn't switched on for your account yet. Contact support.
</Callout>

If the sign-in times out you'll see "Meta connection timed out. Please try again." or "Meta link session expired. Please reconnect." Start the connection again.

## Your connected accounts

Accounts are listed under their profile, showing the platform, @username, a status label and the date it was **Last synced**.

- **Use Social Inbox for DMs**: on eligible accounts, a checkbox that routes DMs through the social inbox instead of directly through Meta.
- **Disconnect** (bin icon): removes the account from Autoch.at. You'll see "Account disconnected successfully". Conversations you already have stay in your inbox.
- **Refresh**: reloads the list.

<Callout kind="tip">
If an account's status shows a problem, for example after a password change or when Meta asks you to re-approve, connect it again using the same steps.
</Callout>

## Meta's rules

Messages sent through Facebook and Instagram follow Meta's messaging policies, including time limits on when you can reply to someone and limits on promotional messages. You are responsible for making sure your AI assistant's replies comply.
