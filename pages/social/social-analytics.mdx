**Social Analytics** shows how your published posts performed across every connected network, so you don't have to check each platform separately.

Find it in the left menu under **Social Media** > **Analytics**. You can also reach it from the **Analytics** button on the [Posts](/pages/social/social-posts) page.

## Filter the data

Across the top:

| Filter | Options |
|---|---|
| **Profile** | **All Profiles**, or one of your [profiles](/pages/social/social-profiles) |
| **Platform** | **All Platforms**, Twitter, Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, TikTok or YouTube |
| **From Date** / **To Date** | Any date range. The page opens on the last 30 days. |
| **Last 7 Days** | A shortcut that sets the dates to the past week |

The numbers update as soon as you change a filter.

## The summary cards

| Card | What it counts |
|---|---|
| **Total Posts** | Posts included in the current results |
| **Impressions** | How many times your posts were shown. One person can see a post more than once. |
| **Engagement** | Likes, shares and comments added together |
| **Clicks** | Clicks on links in your posts |

Below them are separate totals for **Likes**, **Shares** and **Comments**.

## Post Performance table

One row per post and account, with:

- **Platform** and **Account** (@username)
- **Impressions**, **Likes**, **Shares**, **Comments** and **Clicks**
- **Date**

If nothing matches your filters you'll see "No analytics data available". Try widening the dates or choosing **All Platforms**.

## Export

Click **Export CSV** to download the table. The file includes Post ID, Platform, Account, Impressions, Likes, Shares, Comments, Clicks, Views and Date, ready for Excel or Google Sheets. If there's nothing to export you'll see "No data to export".

<Callout kind="info">
Results come from each social network, so very recent posts may show low numbers at first. Check back after a while. The **Sync** button reloads the page; it's not a guaranteed refresh from the networks.
</Callout>

## Tips

- Compare platforms for the same date range to see where your audience engages most.
- Look at your best posts and reuse their style. Try [Caption Generator](/pages/social/caption-generator) to draft something similar.
- High impressions but few clicks usually means the call to action is unclear.
