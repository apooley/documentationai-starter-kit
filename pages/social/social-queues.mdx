---
title: Posting Queues
description: Set weekly posting times for each social profile, then add posts to the queue and let them go out at the next free slot without scheduling each one.
---

A **queue** is a weekly posting schedule for one [profile](/pages/social/social-profiles). For example, "Monday, Wednesday and Friday at 10:00". Add posts to the queue and each goes out at the next free time slot, so you don't have to pick a date for every post.

Find it in the left menu under **Social Media** > **Queues**. The page is titled **Posting Queues**.

<Callout kind="info">
You need at least one profile first. Without one, the page says "No profiles found. Create a profile first." Queues appear in the menu once a profile exists.
</Callout>

## What you'll see

The page shows one card per profile. Each card shows:

- The profile's name, colour and description.
- A green tick when the queue is active, or a grey cross when it's inactive.
- **Status**: **Active** or **Inactive**.
- **Timezone**.
- **Time Slots**: each day and time, such as "Monday at 09:00".

Profiles without a queue show "No queue configured" and a **Create Queue** button.

## Create a queue

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the form">
    Click **Create Queue** at the top, or on the profile's card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the profile">
    Pick the **Profile** the queue is for. A profile has one queue.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the timezone">
    Choose **Timezone**: UTC, Eastern, Central, Mountain or Pacific Time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add time slots">
    Click **+ Add Slot**. Choose a day of the week and a time. Repeat for each slot. Use the bin icon to remove one. You need at least one slot, otherwise you'll see "Please add at least one time slot".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn it on">
    Leave **Queue is active** ticked so it runs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Create Queue**. You'll see "Queue created successfully".
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Add posts to a queue

Posts join a queue from the [Posts](/pages/social/social-posts) page, not from the Queues page:

- When creating a post with no schedule date, click **Create & Add to Queue**.
- On an existing draft, click the **clock icon** (Add to queue).

The post is placed in the next free slot in the queue for the post's profile.

## Change, pause or delete a queue

- **Edit Queue**: change the timezone or slots, then click **Update Queue**.
- **Pause**: edit the queue, untick **Queue is active**, and click **Update Queue**. The card then shows **Inactive**.
- **Delete**: click the red bin icon and confirm ("Are you sure you want to delete this queue?").

<Callout kind="tip">
Consider switching queues to inactive during major news events or a crisis. Automated posts that go out at the wrong moment can look tone-deaf.
</Callout>
