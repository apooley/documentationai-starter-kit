title: Microsoft Dynamics Setup Guide
description: A checklist for connecting Microsoft Dynamics 365 to Autoch.at: what to gather, which connection method to pick, and how to confirm syncing works.
---

This guide walks you through connecting **Microsoft Dynamics 365** (Dataverse) so new clients and conversation notes appear in your CRM. You'll probably want your IT person or Microsoft partner nearby for the Microsoft parts.

The buttons and settings are described in [CRM Connections](/pages/crm/crm-connections). This page is the checklist that comes before it.

<Callout kind="info">
Dynamics is the CRM connection available today. HubSpot and Salesforce are not available yet. If **CRM Connection** isn't in your left menu, it isn't switched on for your account. Contact support.
</Callout>

## Step 1: Gather what you need

| You need | Where to find it |
|---|---|
| **Instance URL** | The web address of your Dynamics environment, for example `https://yourorg.crm.dynamics.com` |
| **Directory ID (Azure AD)** | In the Microsoft Entra (Azure) admin centre, on your tenant's overview page. Also called the "Tenant ID". |
| **A Dynamics account that can sign in and approve access** | Someone with admin rights in your Dynamics environment |
| **Client ID and Client Secret** | Only for the "your own OAuth app" or "Service Principal" methods. Created in Microsoft Entra under **App registrations**. |

## Step 2: Choose a connection method

| Method | Best for | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| **Connect with Microsoft** (hosted) | Most businesses. Simplest. | Enter the Instance URL and Directory ID, then sign in with Microsoft. |
| **Connect with OAuth** (your own app) | If your IT policy requires your own Microsoft app | Enter Client ID, Client Secret, Instance URL and Directory ID, then sign in. |
| **Use Service Principal** | IT-managed, server-to-server access that doesn't depend on one person's login | Enter Client ID, Client Secret, Instance URL and Directory ID. |

The button on your screen depends on how your account is set up. If you see **Connect with Microsoft**, use it.

## Step 3 (own app or Service Principal only): Prepare Microsoft Entra

<Steps>
  <Step title="Register an app">
    In Microsoft Entra, go to **App registrations** and create a new registration. Note the **Application (client) ID** and **Directory (tenant) ID**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give it Dynamics permission">
    Under **API permissions**, add permission for **Dynamics CRM** (shown as Dataverse in some tenants), and grant admin consent if your organisation requires it. Adding only Microsoft Graph permissions is not enough.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a client secret">
    Under **Certificates & secrets**, create a **New client secret** and copy its value straight away. Microsoft only shows it once.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Redirect address (own OAuth app only)">
    If you're using your own app with sign-in, add the redirect address Autoch.at gives you (it ends with `/crm-connection/callback`) under **Authentication**. Ask support for the exact address if you don't have it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Step 4: Connect

Go to **CRM** > **CRM Connection**, find the **Microsoft Dynamics** card and follow the buttons for your method. When it works you'll see "Microsoft Dynamics connected successfully!" and an **Active** label.

## Step 5: Check it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Test Connection">
    Click **Test Connection**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Validate Setup">
    Click **Validate Setup**. Each requirement is listed as ready or needing attention. If it says "Sync needs attention", sign-in is fine but the table you chose needs permission changes in Dynamics. Ask your Dynamics admin.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the table and map fields">
    In **Dynamics table & field mapping**, choose the **Target Dataverse table** (the default is contact) and map any extra fields. Name, email and phone are filled automatically when the table has them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on syncing">
    Tick **Enable Sync**, choose whether to send **Qualified Leads Only**, and keep **Sync Contacts** and **Sync Notes** ticked.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Try a sync">
    Click **Sync Now**, then check the **Sync Logs** at the bottom of the page. A **Success** row means the record reached Dynamics.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Troubleshooting

- **Error or Disconnected:** use the reconnect button on the card. For Service Principal connections, **Reconnect with saved credentials** avoids retyping details.
- **"Needs attention" in Validate Setup:** open the check that isn't ready and follow its description, then run **Validate Setup** again.
- **Notes look cut off:** Dynamics memo fields hold about 10,000 characters. Autoch.at keeps the newest messages and drops the oldest.
