## What makes a qualified lead?

**Qualification Settings** (at **Configuration > AI Assistant > Qualification Settings**) is where you define what a "qualified lead" means for your business. Your AI assistant uses these settings to guide qualification conversations — asking the right questions, collecting the right information, and making the right call about whether a lead meets your criteria.

Getting this right is one of the highest-value things you can do in Autoch.at. A well-configured qualification setup means your sales team only spends time on leads that are actually worth pursuing.

## Qualification fields

Qualification fields are the pieces of information your AI collects from a prospect during a qualification conversation. Common examples include:

- Company name and size
- Industry or vertical
- Budget range
- Timeline to purchase
- Current solution they're using
- Decision-making authority

### Adding a qualification field

1. Go to **AI Assistant > Qualification Settings**.
2. Click **Add Field**.
3. Choose the field type (text, number, dropdown, yes/no).
4. Give it a label (e.g., "Budget Range") and, if it's a dropdown, add the options.
5. Mark it as **Required** if the AI must collect this information before the conversation can be considered complete.
6. Click **Save**.

## Qualification criteria

Qualification criteria are the rules that determine whether a lead is "qualified" based on the information collected. For example:

- Budget is greater than $5,000/month
- Company size is more than 50 employees
- Timeline to purchase is within 6 months

### Required Contact Fields

You can specify which contact details a lead must provide to be considered qualified. 

- **Require Email or Phone**: Use this when either contact method is enough. (This option takes precedence over the individual Email/Phone checkboxes).
- **Require Email**: The lead must provide an email address.
- **Require Phone**: The lead must provide a phone number.
- **Require Company**: The lead must provide a company name.

### Setting up criteria

1. In **AI Assistant > Qualification Settings**, go to the **Criteria** tab.
2. Click **Add Criterion**.
3. Select the field to evaluate, the comparison operator, and the value.
4. Add as many criteria as you need.
5. Choose whether all criteria must be met (AND) or just one (OR).

As the conversation progresses, Autoch.at evaluates the collected data against your criteria in real-time. The current qualification status and extracted data are visible directly in the Conversation Detail view.

## Qualification scoring (optional)

Instead of a simple pass/fail, you can set up a scoring model that assigns points to different answers. For example:

- Budget > $10k/month: 30 points
- Company size > 100 employees: 20 points
- Timeline within 3 months: 25 points

Leads above a certain score threshold are marked as qualified. This gives you more nuance than a binary pass/fail — you can see at a glance how strong a lead is.

## Viewing qualified leads

Once your qualification settings are configured, qualified leads appear in **CRM > Clients** with a "Qualified" badge. You can filter the client list to show only qualified leads and sort by qualification score.

If you've connected a CRM, qualified leads can also be automatically pushed to your CRM as new contacts or deals.

## ADF (Automated Data Forwarding)

When a lead qualifies, Autoch.at can automatically send an ADF (Structured XML Email) to your CRM or lead recipient. ADF is the standard format for delivering lead data to automotive and real estate CRM systems.

### ADF on qualification

The **Send ADF on qualification** toggle (preference key: `structured_xml_email_on_qualification`, default: on) controls whether ADF is sent automatically when a lead qualifies. When enabled, the ADF fires as soon as the lead meets your qualification criteria. When disabled, ADF sends are manual only — you'll need to trigger them from the [Conversation Detail](/pages/conversations/conversation-detail) page using the Resend ADF button.

### Quiet-window delay

After a lead is provisionally qualified, the system waits for a quiet window — a period of conversation silence — before summarizing and sending the ADF. This ensures the conversation has settled and the ADF summary captures the latest context, rather than cutting off a conversation that's still active.

This behavior is automatic. No configuration is needed.

### Deferred ADF until contact info

ADF sales sends are deferred until the lead has an email address or phone number on file. If the lead qualifies but hasn't yet provided contact information, the system waits. Once an email or phone number lands in the lead record, the ADF sends automatically.

<Callout kind="warning">
If contact information is never collected, the ADF will not send. Make sure your qualification flow requires at least an email or phone number, or follow up manually using the Resend ADF button in the [Conversation Detail](/pages/conversations/conversation-detail) view.
</Callout>
