**Qualification Settings** is where you tell Autoch.at what a good lead looks like for your business. Your AI assistant checks each conversation against your criteria, so your team only spends time on leads worth chasing.

Find it in the left menu under **Configuration** > **Qualification Settings**. The page title is **Lead Qualification Settings**.

## How it works

You create one or more **criteria**. Each one can combine:

- **Simple rules**: a minimum number of messages, words that must appear (or must not), and contact details the lead must give.
- **AI judgment**: the AI reads the conversation and gives it a score from 0 to 100 based on a description you write.

When a conversation scores at or above your **Minimum Qualification Score**, the lead is marked qualified.

## Create a criteria

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start">
    Click **New Criteria** (top right). If you have none yet, click **Create Criteria**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it">
    Enter a **Name** such as "High-value lead". Leave **Enabled** ticked to use it straight away.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the priority">
    **Priority** decides which criteria is checked first. Higher numbers go first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add simple rules (optional)">
    Under **Rule-based Criteria**:
    - **Minimum Message Count**: how many messages must be exchanged (starts at 3).
    - **Required Keywords**: click **+ Add Keyword** for each word or phrase the conversation should include.
    - **Disqualification Keywords**: words that rule a lead out.
    - **Required Fields**: tick **Require Email or Phone** (either is enough), or **Require Email**, **Require Phone**, **Require Company**. When **Require Email or Phone** is ticked, the separate Email and Phone boxes are switched off.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add AI scoring (optional)">
    Under **AI-based Criteria**, tick **Use AI Analysis**, then fill in:
    - **AI Prompt**: describe what a qualified lead looks like, in plain English.
    - **Minimum AI Score (0-100)**: the score the AI must give.
    - **AI Weight (0-1)**: how much the AI counts versus your simple rules. 0 means rules only, 1 means AI only, 0.5 is equal. Values between 0.6 and 0.8 work well for most businesses.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the bar">
    **Minimum Qualification Score (0-100)** is the overall score a conversation needs. Start around 60 to 70 and adjust once you see real results.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. You'll see "Qualification criteria created".
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Test before you rely on it

Open a saved criteria with **Edit** and scroll to **Test Criteria** (or click **Test** on the card). Edit the **Sample Messages** and **Sample Contact Data**, then click **Run Test**. You'll see the score, which rules matched and why it did or didn't qualify.

## Manage your criteria

Each card shows its name, whether it's **Enabled** or **Disabled**, its **Priority**, and whether it uses "AI-based evaluation" or "Rule-based evaluation".

- Use the **up/down arrows** to change the order.
- Click **Edit** to change it.
- Click the bin icon to delete it. You'll be asked to confirm, and it can't be undone.

## Send qualified leads onward

- **Email to a sales inbox or CRM system.** In **Settings** > **Notifications**, the **Structured Email (XML)** section has a checkbox, **Send Structured XML on lead qualification**, and **Wait after qualification (minutes)** (0 to 60). With 0, the email goes out immediately. With a higher number, Autoch.at waits for a quiet moment in the conversation, then summarises it and sends.
- **Contact details come first.** Lead emails for sales are held back until the lead has an email address or a phone number. If it never arrives, nothing is sent. Tick **Require Email or Phone** in your criteria, or use **Resend ADF** in [Conversation Detail](/pages/conversations/conversation-detail) once you have it.
- **CRM.** In [CRM Connections](/pages/crm/crm-connections), the **Qualified Leads Only** checkbox limits what's sent to your CRM to qualified conversations.

<Callout kind="tip">
Start with one simple criteria: **Require Email or Phone** plus a Minimum Message Count of 3. Add AI scoring after you've reviewed a week of real conversations.
</Callout>
