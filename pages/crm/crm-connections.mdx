title: CRM Connections

**CRM Connections** sends your clients and conversation notes to the CRM your team already uses, so nobody has to copy and paste. Today the supported business CRM is **Microsoft Dynamics** (Dynamics 365 / Dataverse). You can also link **AutoChat CRM** if your plan includes it.

Find it in the left menu under **CRM** > **CRM Connection**.

<Callout kind="info">
HubSpot and Salesforce connections are not available yet. Please don't plan around them. If the **CRM Connection** menu item is missing, or the page says connections are disabled, CRM connections aren't switched on for your account. Contact support.
</Callout>

## What gets synced

- **Contacts.** Name, email, phone and company for each client.
- **Notes.** Conversation summaries and key insights (qualification status, buying stage and similar) are added to the CRM record.
- **Anything you map yourself.** For Dynamics you can choose exactly which Autoch.at details land in which Dynamics field (see below).

## Connect Microsoft Dynamics

You'll need your Dynamics address (for example `https://yourorg.crm.dynamics.com`) and, depending on the method, your Microsoft Entra (Azure) **Directory ID**. Ask your IT person or Microsoft partner if you're unsure. For a checklist to get ready, see [Dynamics CRM Setup](/pages/crm/dynamics-setup).

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Sign in with Microsoft (simplest)">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Start">
        On the **Microsoft Dynamics** card, click **Connect with Microsoft**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Enter your environment">
        Fill in **Instance URL** and **Directory ID (Azure AD)**, then click **Continue to Microsoft**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Approve">
        Sign in with Microsoft and approve access. You return to Autoch.at and see "Microsoft Dynamics connected successfully!". The card shows **Active** and "Hosted OAuth".
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Your own OAuth app">
    If the button reads **Connect with OAuth**, your account uses your own Microsoft app. Click it, enter **Client ID**, **Client Secret**, **Instance URL** and **Directory ID (Azure AD)**, click **Save OAuth Configuration**, then approve access on the Microsoft page.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Service Principal (advanced)">
    For a server-to-server link that doesn't depend on one person's login: click **Use Service Principal**, enter **Client ID**, **Client Secret**, **Instance URL** and **Directory ID (Azure AD)**, then click **Connect**. Best for IT-managed setups.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Callout kind="alert">
Switching between connection methods later asks you to confirm, because it changes how Autoch.at signs in to Dynamics.
</Callout>

## Check that it works

After connecting, use the buttons on the card:

- **Test Connection** checks that Autoch.at can reach Dynamics.
- **Validate Setup** runs a deeper check and lists each requirement as ready or needing attention. "Needs attention" usually means a sign-in or permission problem. "Sync needs attention" means sign-in is fine but the table you chose needs permission changes; the connection stays active.
- **Sync Now** pushes everything waiting in the queue right away. It's greyed out if **Enable Sync** is off.

## Sync settings

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Enable Sync** | Turns automatic syncing on. Conversations are queued by priority and sent in the background. |
| **Qualified Leads Only** | Only sends conversations that were marked qualified (see [Qualification Settings](/pages/crm/qualification-settings)). With this off, everything except spam is sent. |
| **Enable Bi-directional Sync** | Dynamics only. Also lets changes made in Dynamics flow back. An **Inbound webhook status** box shows whether it's Registered and when the last event arrived. |
| **Sync Contacts** | Sends contact details (name, email, phone, company). |
| **Sync Notes** | Adds conversation summaries and insights as notes. |
| **Update Interval (minutes)** | How often Autoch.at checks for new conversations to sync, from 5 to 1440. Shorter is faster but uses more of your CRM's allowance. 15 to 60 is a good range. |

## Choose the Dynamics table and map fields

Once the connection shows **Active**, a panel called **Dynamics table & field mapping** appears.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick the table">
    Under **Target Dataverse table**, choose where clients should land. The default is **contact**. Use **Search tables** (then **Refresh**) to find others such as lead or account. Click **Save table**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Map a field">
    In the row at the bottom, choose a **Platform field** (what Autoch.at knows), then the **Dynamics field** it should fill, then a **Write mode**, and click **Add**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Remove a mapping">
    Click the bin icon at the end of a mapping row.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="alert">
Changing the target table removes the field mappings you set up for the old table. You'll be asked to confirm first.
</Callout>

**Platform fields you can map:** Email, First name, Last name, Company, Street Address, City, State / Province, Postal Code, Country, **Autochat Summary**, **Autochat Full Text**, **Autochat Notes** and **Workspace Identifier**.

**Write mode:**
- **Replace** overwrites whatever is in the Dynamics field.
- **Append** adds to what's already there. Use it to put **Autochat Notes** into the same memo field as **Autochat Summary** without overwriting the summary.

With no mappings, name, email and phone use safe defaults when the table has matching fields.

<Callout kind="info">
Dynamics memo fields hold about 10,000 characters. If a full conversation is longer, Autoch.at keeps the newest messages and drops the oldest, starting the text with "[Earlier messages omitted to fit CRM field limit.]".
</Callout>

## Watch the sync

The **Sync Logs** table at the bottom lists each sync: **Time**, **CRM**, **Type**, **Client** and **Status**.

- **Success**: your CRM is up to date.
- **Pending**: queued and waiting its turn.
- **Failed**: hover or read the short error. Autoch.at retries automatically. If it keeps failing, check the connection with **Test Connection** or **Validate Setup**.

## Reconnect or disconnect

If a card shows **Error** or **Disconnected**, use the reconnect button that matches how you connected: **Reconnect with Microsoft**, **Reconnect with OAuth** or **Reconnect Service Principal**. For Service Principal connections, **Reconnect with saved credentials** restores access without retyping your environment details.

**Disconnect** stops syncing. Records already in your CRM stay there.
