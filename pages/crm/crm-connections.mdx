## Keep your CRM in sync

**CRM Connections** (at **CRM > CRM Connections**) lets you connect Autoch.at to your existing CRM so that client records, conversation data, and lead qualification results flow between the two systems automatically.

When a customer chats with your AI assistant and gets qualified as a lead, that information can be pushed directly to your CRM — no manual data entry required.

## Supported CRMs

- **Microsoft Dynamics 365** — Entities, contacts, leads, accounts, and annotations
- **HubSpot** — Contacts, companies, deals, and notes
- **Salesforce** — Contacts, leads, accounts, and activities
- **Pipedrive** — Contacts, deals, and notes
- **Zoho CRM** — Contacts, leads, and activities

Don't see your CRM? You can also connect any CRM that supports webhooks using Autoch.at's webhook integration. See [Integrations](/integrations) for details.

## Connecting your CRM

1. Go to **CRM > CRM Connections**.
2. Click **Connect CRM**.
3. Select your CRM provider.
4. You'll be redirected to your CRM's authorization page — log in and grant Autoch.at permission to access your data.
5. Once authorized, you'll be returned to Autoch.at to configure the sync settings.

## Configuring the sync

After connecting, you'll configure what data gets synced and in which direction:

### Contact sync

Choose whether Autoch.at should:
- **Push contacts to CRM** — New clients created in Autoch.at are automatically created in your CRM.
- **Pull contacts from CRM** — Contacts in your CRM are imported into Autoch.at as clients.
- **Bidirectional sync** — Changes in either system are reflected in the other.

### Field mapping

Map the fields in Autoch.at to the corresponding fields in your CRM. For example, you might map Autoch.at's "Company" field to HubSpot's "Company Name" field.

Custom fields you've created in Autoch.at can also be mapped to custom fields in your CRM.

#### Microsoft Dynamics 365 Field Mapping
For Microsoft Dynamics, you have granular control over exactly which Dataverse table (Entity) receives your leads. 
- You can select the target table (e.g., `contact`, `lead`, `account`) and map Autoch.at fields to specific Dynamics attributes.
- **Autochat Notes**: You can map the `autochat_notes` platform field to a Dynamics attribute and set its write mode to **Append** or **Replace**. Using Append allows you to push structured notes into the same memo field as the conversation summary without overwriting it.
- Note: Standard Annotation notes are still attached to the contact/lead/account automatically when supported.

### Conversation sync

Choose whether to sync conversation summaries and notes to your CRM. When enabled, a note is added to the contact's record in your CRM each time a conversation is resolved, summarizing what was discussed.

### Dynamics memo field truncation

When a conversation's full text exceeds the Microsoft Dynamics 10,000 character Memo field limit, Autoch.at uses prefer-recent truncation: it keeps the newest conversation turns and truncates older content. This ensures the most recent context — which is usually the most relevant for your sales team — is always preserved in the CRM record.

<Callout kind="info">
The most recent conversation turns are always kept. Only older messages at the beginning of the conversation are truncated to fit the Dynamics Memo field cap.
</Callout>

## Product feed sync

If your CRM connection includes a product feed (for example, syncing a vehicle inventory or product catalog), Autoch.at periodically syncs the feed to keep your product data current. When a feed sync fails, the system automatically retries with a backoff schedule:

| Retry | Delay after failure |
|---|---|
| 1st retry | 1 hour |
| 2nd retry | 3 hours |
| 3rd retry | 8 hours |
| Subsequent retries | Every 24 hours |

The retry counter resets on the first successful sync. Tenant admins receive an email notification once per failure streak — not on every individual retry — so you're alerted to persistent issues without inbox flooding.

## Monitoring the connection

The CRM Connections page shows the status of your connection and a log of recent sync activity. If a sync fails (for example, because a required field is missing), the error will appear here with details about what went wrong.

<Callout kind="info">
If your CRM connection shows an error, the most common cause is an expired authorization token. Click **Reconnect** to re-authorize and restore the connection. For Microsoft Dynamics, if your connection drops, you can often use the **Reconnect with saved credentials** button to restore access without needing to re-enter your environment details.
</Callout>
