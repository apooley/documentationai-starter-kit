**Clients** is your built-in contact list. Each client is one person, with their name, email, phone, company, the conversations they've had with you, and any notes your team has added.

You'll find it in the left menu under **CRM** > **Clients**.

## How clients get created

- **Automatically.** When a conversation (chat, SMS, email, voice or social) has an email address or phone number for the person, Autoch.at creates a client for them, or links the conversation to the matching client you already have.
- **Anonymous chats don't create clients.** If a visitor never shares an email or phone number, there's nothing to match on, so no client is created.
- **By hand or by import.** You can add clients yourself (see below).

## Find clients

- **Search box.** Type a name, email, phone number or company. The list updates as you type.
- **Tags.** The **Tags:** row under the search box shows one button per tag. Click a tag to show only clients with it; click **All** to clear the filter.
- **Pages.** The list shows 50 clients at a time. Use **Previous** and **Next** at the bottom.

The table shows **Name**, **Email**, **Phone**, **Company** and **Created** for each client.

## Add or edit a client

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the form">
    Click **Create Client** (top right), or click **Edit** at the end of a client's row.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the details">
    Enter **First name**, **Last name**, **Email**, **Phone**, **Company** and **Notes**. All are optional.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Create Client** (new) or **Save Changes** (existing).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Open a client's page

Click a client's name in the list. The page shows:

- Their tags and a **Client ID**.
- Four counters: **Conversations**, **Notes**, **Tags** and **Last conversation**.
- **Combined conversation summary**, an AI-written summary across their conversations (shown when one exists).
- **Conversations**: each linked conversation with its channel and stage. Click **Open** to jump to it in your inbox.
- **Activity**: a timeline of events such as a conversation being linked or unlinked, a client merge, or a conversation closing after inactivity.
- **Notes**: type in **Add a note** and click **Add note**. Use **Delete** to remove a note.

Click **Back to Clients** to return to the list.

## Work with several clients at once

Tick the checkbox beside each client (the box in the header ticks everything on the current page). A bar of bulk actions appears:

- **Export selected** downloads just those clients as a CSV file.
- **Add tags**, **Remove tags** or **Replace tags** opens a list of your tags. Tick the ones you want and click **Apply**.
- **Delete selected** removes them after you confirm.

<Callout kind="alert">
Deleting a client can't be undone. Export a backup first if you're not sure.
</Callout>

## Import clients

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the importer">
    Click **Import**. The **Import Clients** window opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your file">
    Pick a CSV or Excel file (.csv, .xlsx or .xls), then click **Preview file**. You'll see how many rows were found.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Match your columns">
    For each Autoch.at field (Email, Phone, First name, Last name, Company, Street, City, State, ZIP, Country, Date of birth, Notes), choose the column from your file, or leave it as **Not mapped**. Columns whose names match exactly are picked for you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run the import">
    Click **Run import**. You need to map at least **Email** or **Phone**, otherwise the button stays greyed out. When it finishes you'll see how many clients were **Imported**, **Created**, **Updated** and **Skipped**, and a note if any rows had errors.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Export all clients

Click **Export all** to download a CSV of your clients. If you've typed something in the search box, the export includes only the matching clients.

## Merge duplicates

The same person can end up as two clients, for example if they use a work and a personal email.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Look for duplicates">
    Click **Review duplicates**. If any are found, a list of groups appears with the match type and how many clients are in each group (the first 10 are shown).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a group">
    Click **Merge** on a group. The **Merge duplicate clients** window opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose who to keep">
    Choose the **Primary client** (the record you want to keep), tick the **Secondary clients** to fold into it, and optionally add a **Reason (optional)**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Merge">
    Click **Merge clients**. The conversations and history of the others are combined into the primary client.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can also merge from a client's own page using **Merge duplicate group**, when that client is part of a duplicate group.

## Tips

- Add an email or phone number early in a conversation. Your AI assistant can ask for it, and it's what links the conversation to a client. See [Qualification Settings](/pages/crm/qualification-settings).
- Clients you've synced to Microsoft Dynamics keep working here as normal. See [CRM Connections](/pages/crm/crm-connections).
- Import is the quickest way to bring an existing customer list in. Include a header row with the column names.
