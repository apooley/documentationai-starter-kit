title: Conversation Intelligence

**Conversation Intelligence** reads through your conversations and points out three things worth acting on: conversations that went quiet without a follow-up, the objections customers keep raising, and whether faster replies lead to more qualified leads.

Find it in the left menu under **Insights** > **Conversation Intelligence**.

## Choose what you're looking at

Four dropdowns sit at the top right:

| Dropdown | Options |
|---|---|
| Date range | **Last 7 days**, **Last 30 days**, **Last 90 days** |
| Channel | **All channels**, **Chat**, **SMS**, **Email**, **Voice**, **Web Voice** |
| Playbook | **All playbooks**, or any one of your [AI Playbooks](/pages/ai-config/playbooks) |
| Version | **All versions**, or a specific playbook version such as "v3 (active)". Only available once you've picked a playbook. |

Next to them is **Run Analysis**. Click it to refresh the findings now. You'll see "Conversation intelligence run started." Small badges show the date of the **Latest run** and its **Status**.

## Response Time vs Qualification

A bar chart showing how often conversations became qualified leads depending on how fast the first reply arrived. Replies are grouped as **0-5s**, **5-15s**, **15-30s** and **30s+**, with one colour per channel.

Use it to see whether slow replies cost you leads on a particular channel. If the 30s+ bars are much lower, look at your handoff rules and staffing for that channel. If there's nothing to show, you'll see "No response-time data for this range."

## Missed Follow-ups

A list of conversations where a customer was left waiting. Each row shows:

- **Conversation**: click to open it.
- **Channel**
- **Reason**
- **Priority**: high-priority items are shown in red.
- **Last Activity**: when the last message was sent.

Two buttons on each row:

- **Resolve** removes the item once you've dealt with it ("Follow-up resolved.").
- **Suggest refinement** sends the issue to the playbook you selected at the top, so someone can improve it. If no playbook is selected you'll see "Select a playbook to create a refinement suggestion."

If nothing is wrong you'll see "No missed follow-ups detected."

## Objections

The reasons customers push back, such as price or timing. Each row shows:

- **Category** and **Objection**
- **Frequency**: how often it came up.
- **Addressed**: the share of times your assistant handled it.
- **Example**: **View** opens a real conversation where it happened.

Click **Suggest refinement** to send an objection to the selected playbook as an improvement idea ("Refinement suggestion created.").

## A good weekly routine

<Steps>
  <Step title="Refresh">
    Pick **Last 7 days** and click **Run Analysis**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clear follow-ups">
    Work through **Missed Follow-ups**. Open each, reply if needed, then click **Resolve**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Learn from objections">
    Look at the objections with a high **Frequency** and a low **Addressed** percentage. Pick the relevant playbook and click **Suggest refinement**, or add an answer in [Training Context](/pages/ai-config/training-context).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="info">
Only account admins can assign playbooks to assistants. **Suggest refinement** records an idea against the playbook; it doesn't edit the playbook for you.
</Callout>
