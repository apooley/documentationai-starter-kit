**Voice Analytics** is a quick health check for your AI phone line. It summarises calls handled by your AI voice assistant so you can see how busy the line is and whether calls are being completed and recorded.

## How to open it

<Callout kind="alert">
Voice Analytics doesn't have its own item in the left menu yet. To open it, add `/voice-analytics` to the end of your Autoch.at address in the browser (for example `https://your-app-address/voice-analytics`) and press Enter. You can bookmark it.
</Callout>

To read individual calls, listen to recordings or see transcripts, use **Conversations** > **Phone / Voice** in the left menu. See [Voice Conversations](/pages/conversations/voice).

## The four headline cards

| Card | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| **Total Calls** | How many calls your voice assistant has handled. |
| **Completion Rate** | The share of calls that completed. Shows a dash when there are no calls yet. |
| **Avg Duration** | The average call length, in seconds. Very long calls can mean your assistant is struggling with a request. Very short ones can mean callers hang up quickly. |
| **Recording Coverage** | The share of calls that have a recording available. |

## AI Tool Call Performance

**Tool Call Success Rate** shows how often your assistant's actions during a call worked, such as looking up information or booking. A low number is worth investigating: check the connected calendar or other tools in your settings.

## Sentiment Distribution

A simple count of calls by overall caller mood (for example positive or negative). If there isn't enough data yet you'll see "No sentiment data available yet."

## Call Volume Over Time

A table with one row per day: **Date**, **Calls** and **Avg Duration (s)**. Use it to spot your busiest days. If there are no calls you'll see "No voice calls in the selected period."

## Live updates

The page updates by itself while it's open. If the live connection drops, it refreshes about once a minute.

<Callout kind="info">
Recording calls may require you to tell callers they're being recorded, depending on where you and your callers are. Check the rules that apply to you.
</Callout>

## Troubleshooting

- **"Unable to load voice analytics. Please try again."** Refresh the page. If it keeps happening, contact support.
- **All dashes and zeros.** Your voice line hasn't taken calls yet. Set up a number under [Phone Numbers](/pages/settings/phone-numbers) and make a test call.
