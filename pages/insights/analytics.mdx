**Analytics** shows how your AI assistants and team are performing. Use it to spot busy times, see whether your assistants turn conversations into leads, and decide what to improve next.

Find it in the left menu under **Insights** > **Analytics**.

## Choose a time period

Use the dropdown at the top right: **Last 7 Days**, **Last 30 Days** or **Last 90 Days**. Every number and chart on the page updates to match.

Click the green **Export** button to download the data for that period as a CSV file, handy for Excel or sharing with your team.

## The four headline numbers

| Card | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| **Total Conversations** | Every conversation your AI had in the period, across all channels. One thread counts once, however many messages it has. |
| **Qualified Leads** | Conversations that met your [qualification criteria](/pages/crm/qualification-settings). |
| **Conversion Rate** | Qualified leads as a share of all conversations. |
| **Avg Call Duration** | How long calls last on average. |

Hover over the small help icon next to a card for a plain-English explanation.

## Charts

- **Conversations Over Time**: a line for all conversations (blue) and a line for qualified leads (green). Look for busy days, growth and patterns.
- **Channel Distribution** and **Conversations by Channel**: where people reach you (chat, SMS, email, voice, social), with totals, qualified counts and rates per channel.

## Advanced Operational Metrics

This section digs into speed and cost:

- **First Response Time**: how quickly a conversation gets its first reply.
- **Resolution Time**: how long a conversation takes from start to resolved.
- **Agent Response Time**: how quickly your human team replies.
- **CSAT / Satisfaction**: average customer rating and percent satisfied. If no ratings were collected you'll see "No CSAT source data in this window".
- **Cost per Qualified Lead**: your AI spend divided by qualified leads.
- **Conversation Volume by Hour (Heatmap, UTC)**: a grid of weekday by hour. Darker green means busier. Times are in UTC, not your local time.
- **Assistant Performance Comparison**: a table comparing each of your AI assistants on conversations, qualified leads, qualification rate, first response, resolution and cost per qualified lead.

Averages show a "p95" figure beneath them. That's the speed that 95 out of 100 conversations beat, so it highlights your slowest cases. "n" is how many conversations the figure is based on.

<Callout kind="info">
If you see "Partial metric fallback in effect", some advanced numbers couldn't be worked out for that period. The rest of the page is still accurate.
</Callout>

## FAQ Analytics

Shows how well your assistant answers from your [Training Context](/pages/ai-config/training-context):

- **FAQ Hits**: successful matches to a saved answer.
- **FAQ Misses**: attempts with no match.
- **Near-misses**: close, but the answer was rejected as not quite right.
- **FAQ outcomes over time**: a chart of the three above.

Lots of misses means customers are asking things your Training Context doesn't cover. Add answers for those topics.

## Scheduled Reports

Get a summary by email without opening the page.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start">
    In **Scheduled Reports**, click **New Schedule**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set when it sends">
    Give it a **Name**. Choose **Frequency** (Daily, Weekly or Monthly), your **Timezone**, and the **Hour** and **Minute**. Weekly asks for a day of the week; Monthly asks for a day of the month.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add recipients">
    Type an email address under **Recipients** and click **Add**. Repeat for everyone who should receive it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what's included">
    Pick a **Date Range** (Last 7, 30 or 90 days). Under **Include metrics**, tick any of Total conversations, Qualified leads, Conversion rate, Average conversation duration and Channel breakdown. Optionally tick **Include advanced metrics** and **Include time series data**. **Max Attempts** (1 to 10) is how many times Autoch.at tries to deliver if sending fails.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Check the **Preview** (subject line and summary), then click **Save Schedule**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Each schedule shows whether it's **Enabled** or **Disabled**, when it will run next and when it last ran. Buttons on each one:

- **Trigger** sends it right now.
- **Edit** changes the settings.
- **Disable** / **Enable** pauses or resumes it.
- **Delete** removes it.
- **Runs** lists recent sends with the date window, status, attempts, recipients and any error.

## Tips

- Compare 7 days against 30 days to tell a blip from a trend.
- Use the heatmap to plan staffing for human handoffs.
- A falling conversion rate with steady volume often points to your qualification criteria or assistant instructions, not traffic.
