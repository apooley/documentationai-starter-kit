**Chat Widget** controls how the chat bubble looks and behaves on your website, who is allowed to show it, and how to add it to your pages.

Find it in the left menu under **Settings** > **Chat Widget**. It has two tabs: **Configuration** (everything below) and **Tracking**.

Click **Save Widget Configuration** at the bottom of the Configuration tab when you're done. You'll be warned if you try to leave with unsaved changes. To preview changes safely, use [Widget Playground](/pages/conversations/widget-playground).

## Look and feel

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Launcher Style** | **Floating circle** (a round button in the corner), **Bottom-right bar** (a wide bar attached to the bottom right) or **Centre-left bar** (a vertical bar in the middle of the left edge). |
| **Launcher Content** | **Icon only**, **Text only** or **Icon + text**. |
| **Launcher Text** | The label on the launcher when text is shown, for example "Chat with us". |
| **Theme** | **Light** or **Dark** chat window. |
| **Position** | **Bottom Right**, **Bottom Left**, **Top Right** or **Top Left**. |
| **Widget Color** | The main colour for the button, highlights and active states. Use the picker or type a hex code such as `#3B82F6`. |
| **Header Font Colour** | Colour of the company name in the open chat header. Choose a dark colour if your widget colour is light. |

## Greeting, pictures and branding

- **Greeting Message**: the first thing visitors see when the chat opens. Keep it friendly.
- **Avatar URL (optional)**: a picture for your AI assistant. Square images around 200 by 200 pixels work best. The address must be publicly reachable.
- **Header Icon URL (optional)**: a small square icon that replaces the default chat icon in the header.
- **Header Wordmark URL (optional)**: a wide logo image that replaces your company name text in the header.

Your company name and brand colour also come from [Branding](/pages/settings/branding).

### Hide "Powered by"

Tick **Hide "Powered by" disclaimer** to remove the small Autoch.at line at the bottom of the widget. This needs the **White Label** add-on. Without it the box is greyed out and says "White Label add-on required to hide the disclaimer." If it's available for purchase, a **Purchase White Label** button appears next to it.

<Callout kind="alert">
We recommend telling visitors somewhere on your site that they may be talking to AI. You are responsible for any disclosure rules that apply where you operate.
</Callout>

## What visitors must share first

**Contact information** decides what a visitor must enter before they can send their first message:

- **Name only**
- **Name and email**
- **Name and phone number**
- **Name, email, and phone number**

A name is always collected. Asking for an email or phone helps you follow up later, but each extra field may put some visitors off.

### Returning visitors

**Conversation reuse window (hours)** sets how long a returning visitor continues the same conversation. The default is 24. Enter 1 to 168 hours since their last message, or 0 to keep reusing the conversation until the system closes it after it goes quiet.

## Proactive messaging

A proactive message is a small bubble above the chat button that invites visitors to chat. Clicking it opens the chat with that text as the assistant's first message.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn it on">
    Tick **Enable proactive messaging**. If it's greyed out, the feature isn't switched on for your account yet. Contact support.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the message">
    Enter the **Message Template**, for example "Hi! How can we help you today?"
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick how noticeable it is">
    Under **Visibility level** choose **Subtle** (small, gentle fade, good for content pages), **Standard** (balanced, with a notification dot) or **Bold** (large, bouncing, pulsing launcher, good for pricing or checkout pages). A preview shows the result. Open **Advanced visibility** to fine-tune **Bubble size**, **Entrance animation**, **Launcher emphasis**, **Accent style** and **Repeat attention pulse until dismissed**. Changing the level resets those fine-tuned choices.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose when it appears">
    Under **Trigger Conditions** tick any that suit you:
    - **Time delay (seconds)**: after the visitor has been on the page for this long.
    - **Scroll percentage**: after they scroll this far down.
    - **Idle time (seconds)**: after they stop interacting for this long.
    - **Exit intent**: when the mouse heads toward the top of the page, as if leaving.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Optionally react to clicks and hovers">
    Click **Add Custom Event**, choose **Hover**, **Click** or **Focus**, and enter the CSS selector of the element, for example `#pricing-button` or `.cta-link`. Ask your web developer if you need help finding the selector.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Limit repeats">
    **Show only once per session** is ticked by default so visitors aren't pestered. Untick it only if you want the bubble to appear again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Allowed domains

The widget only loads on websites you approve.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add your website">
    Type your domain, for example `example.com`, choose the **Workspace** it belongs to, and click **Add Domain**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add every address you use">
    Add each site or subdomain separately, for example both `example.com` and `shop.example.com`, to be safe.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Each domain shows as Active. Use the dropdown to move it to another workspace, or **Remove** to stop it working.

## Add the widget to your website

<Steps>
  <Step title="Generate a token">
    Click **Generate token**, then **Copy token** if you need it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the snippet">
    Under **Embed snippet**, click **Copy snippet**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste it into your site">
    Paste the code just before the closing `</body>` tag on every page where the chat should appear. If you use WordPress, Shopify, Wix or similar, look for a "custom code" or "footer scripts" area, or send the snippet to your web developer.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="info">
If the widget ever stops appearing, check that the page's domain is in **Allowed Domains**, then generate a fresh token and snippet.
</Callout>

## Tracking tab

Use **Tracking** to send chat outcomes, such as a qualified lead, to your own website analytics. It only covers the embedded chat widget, not SMS, email or voice.

- **Tracking Configuration**: switch on **Enable Chat Widget Tracking**, choose a **PII mode** (how much personal detail may be included), and set **Event Mappings**. Click **Add standard mappings** for the usual ones. You need at least one enabled mapping before outcomes such as Lead Qualified appear.
- **Widget Tracking Health**: shows recent events and failures for the **Last 1 hour**, **Last 24 hours** or **Last 7 days**, split **By Event Type**.
- **Tracking Code Templates**: ready-made snippets for your web developer to paste where your analytics scripts run. Autoch.at doesn't run this code inside the widget.
