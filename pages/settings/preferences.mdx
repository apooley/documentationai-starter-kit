**Preferences** collects everyday workspace and personal options in one place.

Find it in the left menu under **Settings** > **Preferences**.

<Callout kind="info">
Looking for lead alerts, email notifications or structured lead emails (ADF)? Those are under **Settings** > **Notifications**.
</Callout>

## Call Recording

Tick the **Enable call recording** box to record calls handled by your AI phone line. Callers are told the call is recorded for training, quality and compliance. If the box is greyed out, call recording isn't available for your account right now.

<Callout kind="alert">
Recording rules differ by country, state and province. Make sure you meet the consent and notice rules that apply to you and your callers.
</Callout>

## CRM Integration

If your account has access to the AutoChat CRM app, click **Open CRM** to jump into it. If you haven't used it before, your account is created automatically. If you see "CRM access is not enabled for your account", ask your account admin to grant it under [Users](/pages/settings/users).

## Storage Usage

See how much of your storage allowance you've used:

- **Total Storage** with a progress bar, for example "120 MB / 1000 MB".
- **Database**: contacts, conversations and training data.
- **Recordings**: voice call recordings.

The bar turns yellow, then orange, then red as you approach your limit, and a warning appears. Delete recordings or old content you no longer need to free space.

## Setup Wizard

The Setup Wizard walks you through configuring your workspace, creating an assistant, adding knowledge and testing the widget.

- **Open Setup Wizard** starts it, or resumes where you left off.
- **Show Setup Wizard button** shows or hides the floating wizard button on your dashboard.
- **Show contextual help icons** shows or hides the small question-mark helpers next to labels across the app.
- **Reset onboarding progress** clears your wizard progress so you can start again. You'll be asked to confirm. Your button and help-icon choices are kept.

## Phone Numbers

A shortcut card with **Go to Phone Numbers**. See [Phone Numbers](/pages/settings/phone-numbers).

## Keyboard Shortcuts

Tick **Enable Shortcuts** to use keyboard shortcuts for faster navigation. They're off by default so they don't clash with your browser's own shortcuts. Once on, press **g** then a letter to jump around (**g d** Dashboard, **g c** Conversations, **g a** Analytics, **g s** Settings), or **?** to see them all.

## Default Currency

Choose a **Default Currency** and click **Save Default Currency**. Autoch.at uses it for products that are imported without a currency or when a currency is missing during an update. The list includes USD, CAD, EUR, GBP, AUD, NZD and many more.
