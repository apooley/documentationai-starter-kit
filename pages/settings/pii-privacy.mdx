title: PII & Privacy
description: Choose which personal details, like card numbers, emails and phone numbers, are hidden before messages go to AI, and whether personal data is stored.
---

**PII** means personally identifiable information, such as names, phone numbers, email addresses and card numbers. **PII & Privacy** lets you control what personal information is kept in your records and what is hidden before a message is sent to the AI.

Find it in the left menu under **Settings** > **PII & Privacy**.

## PII Storage

Tick **Enable PII Storage** to keep personal details in your client records. If you turn it off, personal details are still hidden from the AI but are not stored in your database.

## PII Redaction Settings

Choose what to hide from the AI. When something is hidden, it is replaced with a placeholder such as [SSN] or [EMAIL] before the message goes to the AI model.

| Setting | What it hides |
|---|---|
| **Redact SSN/SIN** | Social Security and Social Insurance numbers |
| **Redact Credit Card Numbers** | Credit and debit card numbers |
| **Redact Phone Numbers** | Phone numbers in various formats |
| **Redact Email Addresses** | Email addresses |
| **Redact Addresses** | Street addresses |
| **Redact Date of Birth** | Dates of birth |
| **Allow First Name** | When ticked, first names may be included in what's sent to the AI |
| **Allow Last Name** | When ticked, last names may be included if needed |

Tick the items you want covered, then click **Save PII Settings**.

<Callout kind="tip">
If your customers might type in sensitive numbers by accident (card numbers, social security or insurance numbers), tick those redaction boxes. First names are usually fine to leave allowed, so replies can feel personal.
</Callout>

## Contact details are still remembered

Even when redaction is switched off for phone numbers or emails, Autoch.at keeps the phone number or email a visitor types in chat. That way your assistant doesn't ask for it again and your team can follow up. Redaction controls what the AI sees. It doesn't stop contact details being saved for your lead.

## Removing a customer's data

To remove a person from your records, go to **CRM** > **Clients**, find them, and click **Delete** on their row (or tick several and use **Delete selected**). See [Clients](/pages/crm/clients). This can't be undone, and anything already sent to an outside CRM, such as Microsoft Dynamics, has to be removed there separately.

For a formal data-deletion request, or any questions about privacy agreements, contact support.
