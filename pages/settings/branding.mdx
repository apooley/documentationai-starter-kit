title: Branding

---

**Branding** controls how your business appears to customers in your chat widget, emails and other customer-facing messages.

Find it in the left menu under **Settings** > **Branding**. It has two tabs: **Branding** and **Custom Shortlinks**.

## Choose where your branding applies

At the top of the **Branding** tab, **Branding Scope** decides what you're editing:

- **Organization default (all workspaces)**: applies everywhere unless a workspace has its own.
- **A specific workspace**: overrides the default for that workspace only. Any field you leave blank inherits the organization default.

See [Workspaces](/pages/settings/workspaces) if you run more than one brand.

## Set your brand details

<Steps>
  <Step title="Legal Business Name">
    Your registered company name, used in legal documents and invoices. It has its own **Save** button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Company Name (Display)">
    The name customers see in the chat widget header, email signatures and, where supported, as the SMS sender name. It can be different from your legal name, for example a trading name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Logo URL">
    Paste the web address of your logo (PNG, JPG or SVG). It must be publicly reachable, for example `https://example.com/logo.png`. A preview appears underneath. If the preview doesn't show, the address is wrong or the image is private.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Primary Color">
    Pick your brand colour with the colour picker or type a hex code such as `#2563EB`. It's used for widget buttons, links and email templates.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Company Phone Number">
    Shown in the chat widget so customers can reach a person. Use international format, for example +1 (555) 123-4567.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Company Website">
    Your main web address, used in email signatures and the chat widget.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save Branding**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">
Check the result in [Widget Playground](/pages/conversations/widget-playground) (left menu: **Messaging** > **Widget Playground**). More widget options, including greeting text, are in [Chat Widget](/pages/settings/chat-widget).
</Callout>

## Custom Shortlinks

Short links are used in text messages and emails. This tab lets you use your own short web address (for example `example.cc`) instead of the default.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn it on">
    Tick **Enable custom short domain**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the domain">
    Type your **Short Domain** and pick the **Workspace** it belongs to. Click **Save Domain**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up DNS">
    You'll see the address your domain must point to. Add this in your domain provider's DNS settings. This is usually something your web or IT person does.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify">
    After DNS has had time to update, click **Verify DNS**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Secure it">
    When you see "DNS verified!", click **Generate SSL Certificate**. A green message "SSL certificate is active. Your domain is secure." confirms it's ready.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the root redirect">
    Under **Company Website**, enter where visitors go if they open the bare short domain. If left empty they're sent to https://autoch.at. Click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Hiding "Powered by"

To remove the "Powered by" line from your chat widget, use the white-label option in [Chat Widget](/pages/settings/chat-widget).
