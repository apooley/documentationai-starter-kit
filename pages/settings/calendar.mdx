Connect **Calendly** and your AI assistant can offer real appointment times, so customers can book without waiting for someone to reply.

Find it in the left menu under **Settings** > **Calendar**. Calendly is the supported calendar today.

<Callout kind="info">
You need a Calendly account with at least one event type (for example "30-minute consultation"). Create these in Calendly first.
</Callout>

## Connect Calendly

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an access token in Calendly">
    On the Calendar page, follow the **Calendly Integrations settings** link. In Calendly, create a **Personal Access Token** and copy it. It grants access to your calendar and event types.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste it into Autoch.at">
    Back in Autoch.at, paste the token into **Calendly Personal Access Token**. Your token is encrypted and stored securely.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect">
    Click **Connect Calendly**. When it works you'll see **Calendly Connected**: "Your Calendly account is connected and ready to use."
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Set up booking

Once connected, two more areas appear:

- **Enable Appointment Booking**: switch **Enable booking** on to let your AI assistant check availability, suggest meeting times and create appointments in conversations. Switch it off to stop automatic booking while keeping Calendly connected.
- **Default Event Type**: choose which Calendly event type the assistant books by default. It uses this unless the customer asks for a different length. If the list is empty you'll see "No event types available. Please check your Calendly account."

**Available Event Types** lists every event type in your Calendly account with its length in minutes. Click **View** to open its booking page.

<Callout kind="tip">
To change meeting lengths, buffers or working hours, edit them in Calendly. Autoch.at follows whatever availability your Calendly event types allow.
</Callout>

## What the customer sees

When a customer asks to book a time, your assistant can check your availability, offer some options, and confirm the one they choose.

## Disconnect

Click **Disconnect** in the green Calendly box. Your assistant will no longer be able to check availability or book. Appointments already made stay in Calendly.
