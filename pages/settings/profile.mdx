**Profile** is where you manage your own login: your name, business name, email address and password, plus two-step sign-in.

Find it in the left menu under **Settings** > **Profile**. What you change here applies to your own account. The exception is **Business Name**, which is shared across your workspace.

## Update your details

<Steps>
  <Step title="Edit the fields">
    Change your **Name**, your **Business Name** (shown in the sidebar), or your **Email Address**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save Changes**. You'll see "Profile updated successfully".
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Change your email address

<Callout kind="alert">
When you change your email you are signed out straight away and can't sign back in until you confirm the new address. Check your inbox before you start.
</Callout>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Type the new address">
    Replace the text in **Email Address**. A new box appears: **Current password (to confirm it's you)**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your current password">
    Type your current password. If you are also changing your password at the same time, you don't need this box. The **Current Password** box in the Change Password section covers both.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save Changes**. A message says "We've sent a link to your new address. Please confirm it to sign back in." and you are signed out.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Open the email at the new address and click the link. Then sign in with the new email.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Only a real change counts. Capital letters or extra spaces don't trigger the confirmation. Changing your email also signs you out on all your other devices. If an admin changes your email for you, you'll also have to confirm the new address before you can sign in. See [Account Security](/pages/settings/account-security).

## Change your password

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter the passwords">
    In **Change Password**, type your **Current Password**, then your **New Password** (at least 12 characters) and **Confirm New Password**. A strength meter shows how strong it is. Leave all three blank to keep your current password.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save Changes**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

After a password change, you are signed out of every device and must sign in again with the new password.

## Two-step sign-in (MFA)

Under **Security Settings** > **Multi-Factor Authentication**, add a second check when you sign in. The status shows **Disabled** or **Enabled**. Admin accounts especially should turn it on.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Authenticator app (recommended)">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Start">
        Click **Use Authenticator App**, then **Generate QR Code** if needed.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Scan">
        Scan the QR code with an app such as Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, Authy or 1Password. If you can't scan it, type the key shown under "Or enter this key manually".
      </Step>
      <Step title="Verify">
        Enter the 6-digit code from the app and click **Verify & Enable**.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Text message (SMS)">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Start">
        Click **Use SMS** and enter your mobile number with country code, for example +14165551234.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Send the code">
        Click **Send Verification Code**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Verify">
        Enter the 6-digit code from the text message and click **Verify & Enable**.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

### Backup codes

When you finish setting up, you'll see a list of **Backup Codes**. Download or write them down somewhere safe. Each can be used once if you lose your phone, and you can't see them again after you click **I've Saved These Codes**.

At sign-in, tick **Use backup code instead** to enter one.

To get a fresh set, use **Regenerate Backup Codes** and enter your password.

### Turn it off

Under **Disable MFA**, enter your password and click **Disable MFA**. Turning it off signs you out everywhere.

## Signing out

Click **Sign Out** at the bottom of the left menu. Signing out ends your session on all your devices, not just this one.
