title: Email Settings
description: Receive customer emails by forwarding or connecting Gmail or Outlook, and send replies from your own domain with verified DNS records or your SMTP server.
---

**Email** is where you set up how Autoch.at receives customer emails and how replies are sent. The page is titled **Email Configuration**.

Find it in the left menu under **Settings** > **Email**. This item only appears if your plan includes the email channel.

There are two tabs: **Incoming** (how emails reach Autoch.at) and **Outgoing** (how replies are sent).

## Incoming: choose a method

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Forward (simple)">
    Best if you have a support mailbox already.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Copy your address">
        Open the **Incoming** tab and choose **Forward your email to Autoch.at**. Under **Your Incoming Email Address**, click **Copy**. This address is assigned to your account and can't be changed.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Set up forwarding">
        In your email provider (Gmail, Outlook, your domain host), create a forwarding rule that sends your support address (for example support@yourcompany.com) to the copied address.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Tell Autoch.at about the alias (recommended)">
        Under **Reply-To Forwarding Alias**, type the mailbox you forward from and click **Save**. It shows **Unverified** until Autoch.at sees an email arrive from it, then **Verified**. Click **Remove** to clear it.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    New emails then show up in your Conversations inbox. Emails sent to the address are handled by your [email workflows](/pages/conversations/email-workflows).
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Connect Gmail or Outlook (advanced)">
    Lets Autoch.at receive in real time and, optionally, send from the same account.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Start">
        Choose **Connect your email to Autoch.at**, then click **Connect Gmail** or **Connect Outlook**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Sign in and approve">
        Complete the sign-in on the Google or Microsoft page. When you return, the card shows the connected **Email**, its **Status** and the **Last sync**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Disconnect later if needed">
        Use **Disconnect Gmail** or **Disconnect Outlook**.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

### Custom IMAP mailbox (optional)

If you prefer, tick **Use custom IMAP mailbox** and enter the **IMAP Host** (for example imap.gmail.com), **Port** (usually 993), **IMAP Username**, **IMAP Password** and **Mailbox** (usually INBOX). Click **Test IMAP Connection**. Leave a saved password blank to keep it. If you leave this off, Autoch.at uses its default catch-all.

## Outgoing: choose how replies are sent

On the **Outgoing** tab pick one:

- **Use Autochat's system to send mail**: replies go out through Autoch.at's mail system. You must verify your domain first.
- **Connect via SMTP**: use your own mail server, or reuse your Gmail or Outlook connection.

### Verify your own sending domain

Sending from your own domain makes replies look professional and helps them avoid spam folders.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the domain">
    Under **Add Custom Domain**, type your domain without `www` or `http://`, such as `yourcompany.com`, choose the **Workspace**, and click **Add Domain**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the DNS records">
    A yellow **DNS Records Required** list appears. Copy each record into your domain provider's DNS settings. This is the step to hand to your IT person or web host.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify">
    Click **Verify**. **Check Status** refreshes without triggering a check. **Auto-refresh** re-checks every 5 minutes. DNS can take from a few minutes up to 48 hours.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    The domain moves from **Pending Verification** to verified. If you see **Verification Failed**, double-check each record's name and value.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="alert">
Don't start sending from a new domain until it is verified, or your emails may be marked as spam or rejected.
</Callout>

To remove a domain, click **Delete**. You're asked whether to remove its settings from the email service as well. Choose "No" for a temporary removal.

For a verified domain you can click **Create SMTP Credentials** to generate a username and password.

### Use your own SMTP server

Under **SMTP Configuration** enter the host, port, **SMTP Username**, **SMTP Password** and **From Email Address** (if blank, your incoming address is used). **Detect Settings** can fill these in from your email address; click **Apply Settings** to accept. Passwords are stored encrypted, and leaving the password field blank keeps the saved one. Click **Test Connection**, then **Save SMTP Configuration**. A tick-box lets you automatically respond to incoming emails using workflows.
