**Usage & Billing** shows your current plan, how much of it you've used this billing period, your invoices, and the available plans.

Find it in the left menu under **Settings** > **Usage & Billing**. Anyone who can see this page should be someone you trust with your payment details, usually the account owner.

## If you're on a free trial

A blue **Trial Account** banner shows how many days you have left. To keep using Autoch.at after the trial, click a **Subscribe to … /mo** button in the banner or **Subscribe** on a plan card further down.

## Current Plan

Shows your plan name and monthly price, plus what's included:

- **Conversations per month**: each unique conversation counts once, however many messages it has.
- **Voice minutes included**: marked "with Voice add-on" where relevant.
- **Workspaces included**: how many are in your plan, and the monthly price of each extra one. See [Workspaces](/pages/settings/workspaces).
- **SMS, Chat, Email**: your limits. Messages like "SMS not included" or "Unlimited Email" tell you what your plan covers.

### Manage Billing

Click **Manage Billing** to open the secure payment portal. There you can update your card or payment method, see invoices and change your subscription.

## Current Period Usage

This shows what you've used since your billing period began:

| Tile | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Conversations** | Used so far, "of" your plan limit |
| **Voice Min** | Voice call minutes used, "of" your limit |
| **SMS** | Text messages sent and received |
| **Emails** | Emails sent and received |

Below the tiles you'll see:

- **Overage Charges**: if you go over a limit, extra usage is charged at your plan's overage rate and added to your next bill. Details and a **Total Overage** are shown.
- **Base Plan**, **Overage**, and any **Voice add-on workspaces** or **Social add-on workspaces** lines with their monthly cost, then an **Add-ons Subtotal**.
- **Projected Total**: an estimate based on usage so far. The final amount is set at the end of the billing cycle.

If you're close to a limit, consider upgrading before you go over. Compare plans in **Available Plans**.

## Recent Invoices

Your past invoices are listed with **Download PDF** buttons for your accounting. If you have none yet you'll see "No invoices yet".

## Change your plan

At the bottom, **Available Plans** shows each plan's price, conversations, voice minutes, SMS and chat allowance, and email automation. Your plan is marked **Current Plan**. Click **Upgrade** on another plan, or **Subscribe** if you're on trial, and follow the prompts. To downgrade, or for other changes, use **Manage Billing** or contact support.

## Cancel or reactivate

<Steps>
  <Step title="Cancel">
    Click **Cancel Subscription** in the Current Plan box. Confirm with **Yes, Cancel**, or click **Keep Subscription** to go back. Your plan stays active until the end of the current billing period.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Changed your mind?">
    Until the period ends, an orange notice shows the end date and a **Reactivate Subscription** link. Click it to restore your subscription.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="alert">
After a subscription ends your data is kept for a limited time (the screen says 30 days) and may then be deleted. Export anything you need first, for example your clients from the [Clients](/pages/crm/clients) page.
</Callout>
