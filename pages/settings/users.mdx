**Users** is where account admins invite teammates and decide what each person can see and do.

Find it in the left menu under **Settings** > **Users**. Only admins see this item. Everyone else sees "You don't have permission to manage users."

The page is titled **User Management**. Under the title, a line such as "3 of 5 users used" shows how many seats your plan includes.

## Two kinds of user

| Role | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Admin (full access)** | Can do everything, including manage users, settings and billing. Admins automatically have access to the AutoChat CRM. |
| **User (custom permissions)** | Can only do what you allow. You choose by setting permissions one by one or by giving them a role template. |

Give people the least access that lets them do their job. You can change it any time.

## Invite someone

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the form">
    Click **Invite User**. The button is greyed out and you'll see "User limit reached. Upgrade your plan to add more users." if you've used all your seats. See [Usage & Billing](/pages/settings/billing).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter their details">
    Type their **Email** and choose their **Role**: **User (custom permissions)** or **Admin (full access)**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send">
    Click **Send Invitation**. They receive an email with a link to set up their login.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Until they accept, the person shows in the table with the status **invited**. If they didn't get the email, open the pencil menu on their row and click **Resend Invitation**.

## The users table

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **User** | Email address, and who invited them. |
| **Role** | **admin** or **user**. |
| **Role Template** | For regular users, a menu to pick **Custom permissions** or one of your templates. Admins show "System Admin". |
| **Status** | **active**, **invited** or **suspended**. |
| **CRM Access** | A checkbox that lets a regular user open the AutoChat CRM app. Admins always have it. |
| **Last Login** | The date they last signed in, or "Never". |

## Edit a person

Click the pencil icon at the end of their row, then **Edit User**.

- **Email**: see the note below.
- **Role**: **User** or **Admin**.
- **Status**: **Active**, **Invited** or **Suspended**. Suspended people can't sign in.
- **Edit Permissions** (regular users only): tick what they can do, grouped as Conversations, Settings, Users, Analytics, Integrations and Experiments. If the person has a role template, this is switched off. Remove the template first to edit their permissions individually.

Click **Save Changes**.

<Callout kind="alert">
**Changing someone's email address.** The new address has to be confirmed before they can sign in again. They get an email with a link; until they click it they can't log in. If they hadn't accepted their invitation yet, the old invitation stops working, so click **Resend Invitation** to send a new one. Changing or suspending a teammate also signs them out on all their devices. If you change your *own* email here, you'll be signed out a few seconds later and need to confirm the new address too. See [Account Security](/pages/settings/account-security).
</Callout>

## Role templates

A **Custom Role Template** is a saved set of permissions you can give to several people, for example "Support Agent".

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create it">
    In **Custom Role Templates**, click **New Template**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name and choose permissions">
    Enter a **Name**, then tick the permissions in each group. Ticking a group's top box selects everything in it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and assign">
    Save it. Then, in the users table, pick the template in that person's **Role Template** menu.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Each template shows how many people it's assigned to. **Edit** changes it for everyone who has it. **Delete** removes it. If it's still in use you'll be asked whether to unassign those people and keep their current permissions before deleting.

## CRM access

Tick **CRM Access** to let a regular user open the AutoChat CRM app (clients, invoices and projects). Untick it to take access away.

## Remove a person

Open the pencil menu on their row and click **Delete**, then confirm. You can't delete yourself. Deleting can't be undone. If you only want to stop someone signing in for now, set their **Status** to **Suspended** instead.
