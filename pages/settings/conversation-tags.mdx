**Conversation tags** are coloured labels you can add to a conversation, such as "Urgent", "Billing" or "VIP". They help your team sort and find conversations quickly.

Find the tag library in the left menu under **Settings** > **Conversation Tags**.

## Create a tag

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start">
    Click **New Tag**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name and colour">
    Type a **Tag name**, such as "Urgent". Pick a **Color** with the colour picker or type a hex code (for example `#64748B`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Create Tag**. You'll see "Tag created".
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Manage your tags

The page shows every tag as a coloured badge with its colour code. Use **Search tags** to filter the list. A counter at the bottom reads "Showing N of M".

- Click the **pencil** to rename a tag or change its colour, then **Save Changes**.
- Click the **bin** to delete a tag.

<Callout kind="alert">
Deleting happens straight away, without a confirmation message.
</Callout>

## Add tags to a conversation

Open a conversation and use its **Tags** area to choose the tags to apply. A conversation can have several tags.

## Filter by tag

On the conversation lists, such as chat and SMS, use the tag filter to show only conversations with a given tag. This is handy for reviewing all "Billing" conversations at the end of the day, or checking everything marked "Escalated".

## Conversation tags and client tags

Conversation tags label a **conversation**. Client tags label a **person** on the [Clients](/pages/crm/clients) page. They are separate lists.

## Tips

- Keep your list short and clear. Too many similar tags make filtering less useful.
- Choose distinct colours for the tags you look at most.
- Agree names as a team, for example "Billing" not "billing issue" and "Billing Q".
