A **canned response** is a saved reply for questions you answer again and again, such as opening hours or your refund policy. Your team types a shortcut and the full reply appears in the message box, ready to edit and send.

<Callout kind="alert">
There isn't a Canned Responses link in the left menu yet. To open the page, add `/settings/canned-responses` to the end of your Autoch.at address in your browser (for example `https://your-app-address/settings/canned-responses`) and press Enter. Bookmark it for next time.
</Callout>

## Create a canned response

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the form">
    Click **New Response**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it">
    Enter a **Name** for your own reference, such as "Follow up".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a shortcut">
    Enter a **Shortcut**, such as `follow_up`. The form reminds you: "Use in compose with /shortcut". Keep it short and easy to remember.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the reply">
    Type the message in **Content**. Use these placeholders and they're filled in automatically when the reply is used:
    - `{{customer_name}}`
    - `{{company_name}}`
    - `{{channel}}`

    For example: `Hi {{customer_name}}, just checking in...` Any other `{{...}}` text is left as you typed it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Create**. All three fields are required.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Use one in a conversation

1. Open a conversation and click in the reply box.
2. Type `/` followed by the first letters of the shortcut or name. A list of matches appears.
3. Pick the one you want, using the arrow keys and Enter or by clicking it. The full text is inserted.
4. Edit it if you like, then send.

## Find, edit and delete

The page lists each response by **Name**, **Shortcut** (shown as `/shortcut`) and **Content**, in alphabetical order. Use **Search by name or shortcut** to filter. The first 50 matches are shown, with a count such as "Showing 12 of 12".

- Click the **pencil** to edit, then **Save Changes**.
- Click the **bin** to delete it.

## Tips

- Use a placeholder for the customer's name so replies feel personal.
- Review your list now and then. Remove old replies with out-of-date prices or hours.
- Keep one idea per response so it's easy to mix and match.
