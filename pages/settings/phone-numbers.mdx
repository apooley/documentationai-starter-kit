A business phone number lets customers text you and call your AI voice assistant. **Phone Numbers** shows whether you have one and lets you get one.

Find it in the left menu under **Settings** > **Phone Numbers**. This item only appears when your plan includes SMS.

## Check your status

The **Current Status** box tells you where you stand:

| Message | What it means |
|---|---|
| **You have an active phone number configured.** | You're set. The button reads **Phone Number Active**. |
| **No phone number provisioned yet.** | You don't have one yet. |
| **SMS not enabled on your plan. Upgrade to enable phone provisioning.** | Your plan doesn't include phone numbers. See [Usage & Billing](/pages/settings/billing). |
| **Quota exceeded: X of Y numbers used.** | You've used all the numbers your plan allows. |
| **Eligible to provision a phone number.** | You can get one now. |

## Get a phone number

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start">
    On the **Phone Numbers** page, under **Provision Phone Number**, click **Provision Phone Number**. It opens the **Phone Number Provisioning** page, which shows how many numbers your plan allows and how many are active. Click **Refresh** if those look out of date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter an area code">
    Type the 3-digit **Area code**, for example 415. Autoch.at searches for an available number in that area.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Provision">
    Click **Provision Phone Number**. When it finishes you'll see "Phone number provisioned" followed by your new number.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Getting a number is optional. Many businesses start with chat and email and add a phone line later.

<Callout kind="info">
If the page says "Phone Provisioning Temporarily Disabled", numbers can't be ordered right now. Check back later or contact support.
</Callout>

## Using your number

Once you have a number, texts sent to it arrive in your Conversations inbox (**SMS Conversations**) and calls appear under **Phone / Voice**. See [SMS Workflows](/pages/conversations/sms-workflows) and [Voice Conversations](/pages/conversations/voice). Review call performance in [Voice Analytics](/pages/insights/voice-analytics).

## Changing or releasing a number

There's no button for renaming or releasing a number on this page. If you want to change or give up your number, contact support.
