A **workspace** is a separate area inside your Autoch.at account, with its own assistants, conversations and settings. Most businesses need just one. Add more if you run several brands, locations or clients and want to keep them apart.

Find it in the left menu under **Settings** > **Workspaces**.

## Switch between workspaces

Use the workspace switcher in the left sidebar to choose which workspace you're working in. Pages that depend on a workspace, such as Social Media, then show that workspace's information. Your choice is remembered in this browser. It does not change your account's **Default** workspace.

## Your workspace allowance

A blue box at the top shows:

| Item | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Active workspaces** | How many you have now |
| **Available slots** | How many you can have |
| **Included in plan** | Slots that come with your plan |
| **Paid workspaces** | Extra slots you've bought |
| **Price per workspace** | The monthly price for each extra slot |

### Buy more slots

When you reach the limit you'll see **Workspace limit reached**. Enter how many to add (1 to 10) and click **Purchase N Workspace(s)**. The monthly price is shown on the button. You can also buy ahead of time from the **Purchase Additional Workspaces** box.

## Create a workspace

<Steps>
  <Step title="Name it">
    Under **Create New Workspace**, type a **Workspace Name**, such as "Downtown Clinic".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Optional details">
    **Slug** is a short web-friendly name like `downtown-clinic`. **Workspace Identifier** is free text, up to 100 characters, such as "Clinic A Website". It can be sent to your CRM so you can see which workspace a lead came from.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create">
    Click **Create Workspace**. You'll see "Workspace created successfully". It starts empty, so set up its assistants and channels separately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Manage existing workspaces

Each workspace appears as a card showing its name, **Slug** and **Identifier**.

- **Set Default** makes it your account's default. The default workspace is used for new sessions and records when no workspace has been chosen yet. It's marked with a **Default** badge.
- **Rename** lets you change the **Workspace Name**, **Slug** and **Workspace Identifier**.
- **Delete** removes it (not available for the default). You'll choose between **Cancel billing and delete workspace** and **Keep workspace slot for future use**.
- **Assign unassigned to default** (near the top) moves any records that don't belong to a workspace into the default one. It doesn't change the workspace you're viewing.

<Callout kind="alert">
Deleting a workspace removes the setup that lives in it. Make sure you've finished with it first.
</Callout>

## Workspace add-ons: Voice and Social

At the bottom of each workspace card, **Workspace Add-ons** has a checkbox for each:

- **Voice**: AI phone calls.
- **Social**: unlocks the **Social Media** menu (profiles, accounts, posts, queues, analytics) for that workspace.

Each shows its status (**Active** or **Pending**) and monthly price. Other labels you may see:

- **Grandfathered (no charge)**: included for your account.
- **Coming Soon**: not yet available to purchase.
- **Active paid plan required to enable add-ons.**: use **Go to Billing** to subscribe first.
