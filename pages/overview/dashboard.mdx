# Dashboard

The Dashboard is the first screen you see after signing in. It shows how your business is doing at a glance and gives you shortcuts to the things you do most.

<Callout kind="info">
What you see depends on your plan and on the workspace you have selected in the sidebar. For example, voice call numbers only appear if your plan includes voice.
</Callout>

## Trial banner

While you are on a free trial, a banner shows how many days are left. Use it to go to **Usage & Billing** and subscribe before the trial ends so nothing is interrupted. See [Usage & Billing](/pages/settings/billing).

## Finish setting up

If you have not finished the Setup Wizard, a reminder tells you how many setup steps are left. Click **Resume Setup Wizard** to carry on where you stopped, or dismiss the reminder. You can reopen the wizard at any time from **Settings > Preferences**. The steps are described in the [Quickstart](/quickstart).

A **Get Started** panel also appears on brand-new accounts to point you to the first useful things to do.

## Key numbers

The cards at the top summarise the period you are viewing:

- **Total Conversations**: how many conversations you have had across your channels.
- **Qualified Leads**: how many people the AI (or your team) marked as good leads.
- **Voice Calls**: phone calls handled. Shown only if your plan includes voice.
- **Active Channels**: how many of your channels are switched on and working.

For deeper reports, open [Analytics](/pages/insights/analytics).

## AI Intelligence

This panel shows short, plain-English insights about your conversations, such as what customers keep asking about. If there are none yet, click **Generate Insights** to create them. You can dismiss insights you do not need. For fuller analysis, see [Conversation Intelligence](/pages/insights/conversation-intelligence).

## Recent Activity

A list of what has happened lately, such as new conversations and qualified leads. Click an item to jump to it.

## Quick Actions

Shortcuts to the places you visit most:

- **Conversations** by channel (chat, SMS, email and voice), each with a count so you can see where messages are waiting.
- **Assistants**: view and edit your AI assistants. See [Assistants](/pages/ai-config/assistants).
- **Training assets**: add or update what your assistant knows. See [Training Context](/pages/ai-config/training-context).
- **Analytics**: open detailed reports.
- **Documentation**: opens this help site in a new tab.

## System Status

Shows which of your channels are ready. SMS and Voice show **Active** once you have a phone number set up, or a **Setup** prompt that takes you to the right settings page. Email and the chat widget are covered the same way. See [Phone Numbers](/pages/settings/phone-numbers), [Email](/pages/settings/email) and [Chat Widget](/pages/settings/chat-widget).

## Tips

- Use the workspace switcher in the sidebar to see another workspace's numbers. See [Workspaces](/pages/settings/workspaces).
- If a card looks empty, the most likely reason is that the channel is not set up yet. Check System Status.
