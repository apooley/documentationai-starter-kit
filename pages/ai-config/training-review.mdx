---
title: Training Review and Training Mode
description: Teach your AI from real chats. Flag good and bad conversations with chat commands, filter them in Conversations, and run an AI-guided Training review.
---

You can improve your AI by showing it what a good chat and a bad chat look like. There are three parts: **training mode** (flagging chats), **filtering** the flagged chats, and the **Training review** debrief.

<Callout kind="info">
Training mode works on **website chat**. The Review button needs AI Playbooks to be turned on for your account.
</Callout>

## 1. Turn on training mode for an assistant

1. Go to **AI Assistant → Assistants** and click **Edit** on the assistant.
2. In **Channels & Routing**, switch on **Training mode (web chat)**.
3. Save.

In the [Widget Playground](/pages/conversations/widget-playground) you'll now see "Training mode enabled for this assistant," and the chat box shows a reminder of the commands.

<Callout kind="alert">
Use training mode on a test or internal assistant. Anyone chatting with an assistant that has it on can use the commands.
</Callout>

## 2. Flag chats with commands

Type one of these into the chat box:

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/flag-good` | Marks the conversation as a good example. |
| `/flag-bad` | Marks it as a bad example. |
| `/clear` | Resets the widget session so you can start as a brand-new visitor. It doesn't mark anything and nothing is sent to the AI. |

## 3. Find flagged chats

In [Conversations](/pages/conversations/conversations):

- A **green** or **red flag** icon appears beside flagged conversations.
- Open **Filters** and set **Training review** to **All**, **Good** or **Bad**.
- On a conversation's page, a **Training: Good** or **Training: Bad** badge shows in the header.

## 4. Run a Training review

On a **chat** conversation's page, click **Review** (next to **Transcript**).

1. An AI interviewer asks you about the conversation. Answer in the box and press Enter.
2. When it's done you'll see a **Review summary**, **Proposed playbook changes**, **Proposed assistant changes** and **Additional guidance**.
3. Click **Apply playbook changes** (you can tick **Activate new playbook version immediately**), **Apply assistant changes**, or **Close** to do nothing.

<Callout kind="alert">
Playbook changes affect every assistant using that playbook. Assistant changes affect only this assistant.
</Callout>
