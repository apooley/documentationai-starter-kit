An **assistant** is an AI team member that talks to your customers. You can have as many as you need, for example a Support assistant, a Sales assistant and a Front-desk assistant, each with its own personality, knowledge and channels.

Find them under **AI Assistant → Assistants** in the left sidebar.

## The Assistants page

Each assistant appears as a card showing:

- Its name (your **Internal Name** if you set one, with the **Public name** underneath).
- Badges for the channels it is the default for: **Default**, **Email**, **SMS**, **Chat & DMs**, **Voice**.
- Its **Workspace**, its linked **Playbook**, and, if warm transfer is on, a **Transfers to:** line.
- Buttons: **Rules**, **Edit**, **Clone** and **Delete**.

Use the **Search assistants...** box to find one. The star at the top-right of a card (**Set as default**) makes it your overall default assistant. The **Routing Rules** button at the top opens [Routing & Handoffs](/pages/ai-config/routing-handoffs).

<Callout kind="info">
Only assistants in your current workspace are shown. Switch workspace from the sidebar to see the others.
</Callout>

## Create an assistant

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Create Assistant">
    On the Assistants page, click **Create Assistant**. Tick the channels you plan to use (Email, Chat, Voice, and SMS if your plan has it).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose how to set it up">
    - **AI-assisted setup**: answer a few questions and the AI drafts the instructions for you.
    - **Quick setup**: pick a goal (Sales / Lead qualification, Customer support, Scheduling or Other), add a short goal description and tone, and create it.
    - **Full manual setup**: fill in every setting yourself, using the steps below.

    You can also start from a ready-made template.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Work through the setup steps">
    When creating a full assistant you may see a step bar at the top. The steps are **1. Basics**, **2. Channels & Routing**, **3. Knowledge**, **4. Behavior** and **5. Review**. You can click any step to jump to it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Create Assistant**. If something needs fixing, the message appears inside the pop-up, so you won't lose your work.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Basics

| Field | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Name** (required) | The name customers see. It is also used in email signatures. |
| **Internal Name (recommended)** | A name only your team sees, to tell assistants apart. If you leave it blank, the Name is used. |
| **Workspace** | Which workspace the assistant belongs to. Used for routing defaults and branding. |
| **Description** | A short note about what this assistant is for. |
| **Playbook (recommended)** | Optional on create. Pick the playbook this assistant should follow. You can change it later. Only admins can change it. |

### Behavior

- **Tone**: Friendly, Professional, Concise, Empathetic, Upbeat, Casual, Salesy, or **Other** (describe it in up to 120 characters).
- **Role**: Not set, Sales, Support or Hybrid. This shapes the assistant's goals and when it offers a handoff.
- **Playbook**: the single playbook this assistant follows on every channel. See [AI Playbooks](/pages/ai-config/playbooks).
- **Primary KPIs**, **Required Fields to Capture** and **Escalation Triggers**: one item per line, for example "name", "email", "User asks for a human".
- **Next Step Guidance**: what the assistant should offer at the end, for example "Offer to book a demo or send a quote."
- **Offer Handoff After (attempts)**: how many tries before the assistant offers a person.
- **System Prompt** (required): the main instructions that define who the assistant is and how it behaves.
- **Greeting Message**: the first thing it says, for example "Hello! How can I help you today?"
- **Email Signature (Optional)**: text added after the assistant's name in emails.

<Callout kind="tip">
Keep the System Prompt about *behavior* ("You are a friendly, concise support agent for Acme..."). Put facts about your business in [Training Context](/pages/ai-config/training-context) instead.
</Callout>

### Channels & Routing

- **Provider** and **Model**: the AI engine behind the assistant. Most businesses leave the defaults. You only see Provider choices if your account uses its own AI keys.
- **Voice**: the voice used on phone calls.
- **Routing default (fallback for all channels)** plus **Default for Email**, **Default for SMS**, **Default for Chat & DMs** and **Default for Voice**. A channel-specific default wins over the overall default.
- **Training mode (web chat)**: lets testers type /clear, /flag-good and /flag-bad in the chat widget. Use it only on a test assistant.
- **Use Shortlinks**: turns links in messages into tracked short links. Handy for email and SMS.

If you give several assistants the same channel, use [routing rules](/pages/ai-config/routing-handoffs) to decide which one answers.

### Knowledge

Under **Context Assignments**, **Include all context** (the default) lets the assistant use everything in your [Training Context](/pages/ai-config/training-context): documents, products, website content and instructions. Untick it to pick specific items.

### Advanced Options

Click **Advanced Options** to expand:

- **OpenAI Temperature** (0 to 2, default 0.7): lower is more consistent, higher is more varied.
- **Max Tokens** (default 2000): caps how long replies can be. Keep it lower for chat and SMS.
- **Topic Boundaries**: topics to stay within, one per line.
- **Do Not Instructions**: things it must never do or say.
- **Query Result Limits (Per-Agent Override)**: optional caps on how much material this assistant pulls in per question: **Max Products Per Query**, **Max Documents Per Query**, **Max Website Pages Per Query**, **Max Product Description Length**, **Max Document Preview Length** and **Max Website Preview Length**. Leave them empty to use your plan's defaults.

## Link a playbook

An assistant follows one playbook at a time.

1. Open the assistant with **Edit**.
2. In **Behavior**, choose a playbook in the **Playbook** list (or **No playbook**).
3. Save.

You can also assign a playbook from the **AI Playbooks** page, which shows which assistants use each one.

<Callout kind="info">
Playbook linking is admin-only. If you are not an admin you will see the playbook name with "Ask an admin to change this."
</Callout>

## Hand chats to a colleague (warm transfer)

On website chat, an assistant can pass a visitor to a better-suited assistant. The visitor sees "Connecting you with *name*…" and the next assistant gets a short summary of the conversation.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the assistant">
    Click **Edit** on the card (warm transfer is shown when editing an existing assistant).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn it on">
    In **Behavior**, switch on **Warm transfer (website chat)**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick colleagues">
    Under **Can transfer to**, tick up to 10 other assistants in the same workspace. Paused assistants are not listed. Give each colleague a clear description so the AI picks the right one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check handoffs are on">
    Transfers only work if handoffs are on for your business. If they're off, the editor shows a warning with a link to **Routing & Handoffs → Handoff Settings**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

See [Warm transfer between assistants](/pages/ai-config/warm-transfer) for more.

## Clone an assistant

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Clone">
    On the assistant's card, click **Clone**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it and pick a workspace">
    Enter a **New name** and choose a **Target workspace**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decide about the playbook">
    Tick **Also clone linked playbook** to make a separate copy (give it a **New playbook name**). If you leave it unticked, the copy keeps using the same playbook.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click **Clone**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="alert">
Default settings are not copied. Set the new assistant's channel defaults yourself.
</Callout>

## Delete an assistant

Click **Delete** on the card, then **Delete assistant** to confirm. This cannot be undone.

- Its routing rules, instructions and email assignments are deleted with it.
- If it was a channel's default, choose another default afterwards.
- Past conversations are kept, but no longer show which assistant handled them.

## Test before you go live

- Use the **Widget Playground** (sidebar → **Conversations → Widget Playground**) to chat with your assistant as a customer would. See [Widget Playground](/pages/conversations/widget-playground).
- In the editor, **Test prompt**, **Test Email** and **Test SMS** let you try a message, and **Run test scenarios** checks the assistant against realistic requests.
- **Quality Check** (on the Review step) checks your instructions and lists issues with suggested fixes.
