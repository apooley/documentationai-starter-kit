---
title: Training Context
description: Teach your AI assistants about your business with products, documents, website pages, instructions and FAQs, then choose which assistants use what.
---

An assistant's System Prompt tells it *how to behave*. **Training Context** tells it *what to say*: your products, prices, policies and answers. Without it, the AI only knows general information and can't answer questions about your business.

Open **AI Assistant → Training Context** in the sidebar. Content is kept per workspace; the page tells you which workspace you're viewing ("Showing content for workspace…"). Change it from the sidebar.

<Callout kind="info">
Training Context appears in the sidebar only when it is turned on for your account.
</Callout>

## The tabs

| Tab | Use it for |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | See how much of your plan's training space you've used. |
| **Products** | Your catalog: what you sell, prices, features, availability. |
| **Documents** | Manuals, policies, guides. |
| **UI Screens** | Structured files that describe specific screens in your app. |
| **Website** | Pages pulled in from your website. |
| **Instructions** | Business rules and special cases. |
| **FAQs** | Question-and-answer pairs. |

On a narrow screen the tabs turn into a **Training context section** drop-down.

## Overview: how much space you have

The Overview shows **characters used / your plan's limit** and a percentage bar that turns yellow at 80%, orange at 90% and red at 100%. Click **Refresh** to recalculate. If you're close to the limit, delete outdated items or combine documents.

## Products

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Products tab">
    Click **Import Products**, or **Add Product** to type one in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Format and Import Type">
    **Format**: CSV (you'll match your columns to product fields) or JSON (no matching needed). **Import Type**: **Upload File** or **Remote URL** (a public link to a CSV or JSON file).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add helpful details">
    **Context (Optional)** is a sentence about the data, like "These are our current products and pricing." **Default Currency** is used for products with no currency.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Import">
    Follow the prompts. Later, use **Sync** to pull a fresh copy from a feed URL. If a sync fails you'll see the reason and a **Retry sync** button. **Delete Feed** removes the feed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">
Keep your catalog current. When the assistant shares a product link in chat, it checks your catalog first, so it only links to products that exist. Products missing from the catalog can't be linked.
</Callout>

## Documents

Click **Upload** for one file or **Bulk Upload** for several. Accepted types: PDF, DOCX, TXT, MD and XLSX. Use **New Folder** and **Move to folder** to organize, and switch between **Folder View** and **List View**.

## UI Screens

Upload structured UI Screens files so the AI can answer questions about specific screens without mixing them into your general documents.

## Website

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter your Base URL">
    Type your site address, such as `https://example.com`, and click **Save**. The crawler follows links from this page and stays on the same domain.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Crawl">
    A progress banner shows pages crawled. When it finishes, you'll see "N pages crawled".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fine-tune (optional)">
    Open **Advanced Settings** to tick **Ignore query strings and trailing slashes**, add **Exclude Paths** (like `/admin/*`) and **Exclude CSS Selectors** (like `.sidebar` or `#footer`).
  </Step>
</Steps>

After the first crawl, use **Re-crawl Website** to pick up changes, **Refresh Selected** (or **Refresh All**) to refresh specific pages, and **Regenerate Summaries** for pages missing a summary. **Crawl & Sources** and **Organize & Folders** switch between crawling and tidying your pages.

## Instructions

Short rules for your AI, like "Always mention our 30-day guarantee." Click **Add Instruction** and fill in:

- **Title** and **Content**
- **Type**: System, Product or Behavior
- **Priority**: a number. Use it to rank which instruction matters more.

Click **Create**. Use **Edit** to change one later.

## FAQs

FAQs are exact question-and-answer pairs. The AI uses them when a customer's question closely matches. FAQs must be **published** to be used.

- **Add FAQ**: type a question and answer.
- **Import CSV** (use **Download template** for the format) and **Export CSV**.
- **Import from URL**: paste an FAQ page link, or paste the page's HTML, review the questions found, then import. They arrive as drafts.
- Select FAQs and click **Publish selected** to make them live.

<Callout kind="alert">
If a site uses Cloudflare bot protection, URL import may be blocked. Use CSV import instead.
</Callout>

## Choose which assistants use what

By default an assistant can use everything. On any tab, select items and click **Add to assistant** (or **Add to assistants**) to give them to specific assistants, then click **Assign**. You can also pick items inside an assistant's **Context Assignments** (see [AI Assistants](/pages/ai-config/assistants)).

You can also **Delete selected** items in bulk, and use the page search to find an item.

## Keep it fresh

If a customer gets a wrong answer, check Training Context first. Outdated or conflicting content is the most common cause. After pricing, policy or product changes: edit or re-sync the item, re-crawl the website, and delete anything no longer true.

## Training Context vs. Messaging Directives

| | |
|---|---|
| **Training Context** | *Facts*: policies, product details, answers. |
| **Messaging Directives** | *Style rules*: how the AI communicates, whatever the topic. |

Want the AI to know your return policy? Add it here. Want it to always end with a question? Use [Messaging Directives](/pages/ai-config/messaging-directives).
