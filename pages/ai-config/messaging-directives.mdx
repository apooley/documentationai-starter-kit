title: Messaging Directives
description: Write account-wide rules for how your AI talks to customers. Set a central directive, add chat, email or SMS rules, and define role goals and handoff guidance.
---

**Messaging Directives** are standing instructions that apply to your AI across the whole account. Use them for things that should be true no matter which assistant is answering, such as your brand voice, a required disclaimer, or how long replies should be.

Open **AI Assistant → Messaging Directives** in the sidebar.

<Callout kind="info">
Directives are saved for your whole account, not for a single workspace or assistant. Anything you write here is combined with each assistant's own System Prompt.
</Callout>

## The five tabs

| Tab | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Central Directive** | One set of instructions for all channels: chat, email and SMS. |
| **Role & Objectives** | The AI's role, what it should achieve, and when to offer a person. |
| **Chat** | Extra rules for website chat only. |
| **Email** | Extra rules for email only. |
| **SMS** | Extra rules for text messages only. |

Each tab has its own save button, so saving one tab never changes another.

## Write a central directive

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Central Directive tab">
    This is the first tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write your instructions">
    Type into the **Central Directive** box. Describe the AI's core personality, goals and general behavior, for example "You are a helpful assistant for Acme Co. Keep replies under three sentences and always end with a question."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save Central Directive**. You'll see a green confirmation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">
Write rules as direct instructions, not wishes. "Keep responses under 3 sentences" works better than "Try to keep responses fairly short." Be specific.
</Callout>

## Add rules for one channel

Customers expect different things on different channels. For example, email can be longer and more formal, and SMS should be brief.

1. Open the **Chat**, **Email** or **SMS** tab.
2. Type the extra rules in the **CHAT Directive**, **EMAIL Directive** or **SMS Directive** box.
3. Check the **Preview** box underneath. It shows your central directive plus the channel rules, exactly as combined.
4. Click **Save CHAT Directive** (or EMAIL / SMS).

Channel rules are added to the central directive; they don't replace it.

## Role & Objectives

Use this tab to tell the AI what job it is doing and what success looks like. The values are added to the AI's instructions as a checklist.

| Field | What to enter |
|---|---|
| **Role** | Not set, Sales, Support or Hybrid. |
| **Primary KPIs (one per line)** | What counts as a win, for example `book_demo` or `qualify_lead`. |
| **Required Fields to Capture (one per line)** | Details the AI should collect, for example `name`, `email`, `phone`. |
| **Escalation Triggers (one per line)** | When to bring in a person, for example "User asks for a human" or "Out-of-scope request". |
| **Next Step Guidance** | What the AI should offer next, for example "Offer to book a demo or send a quote." |
| **Offer Handoff After (attempts)** | A number. After this many tries, the AI offers to connect the customer with a person. |

Click **Save Role Objectives** when you're done.

<Callout kind="info">
Each assistant's own editor has similar Role and handoff fields (see [AI Assistants](/pages/ai-config/assistants)). Keep them consistent so the AI isn't getting mixed messages. When a person actually takes over is controlled in [Routing & Handoffs](/pages/ai-config/routing-handoffs).
</Callout>

## Good things to put in a directive

- **Format:** "Never use bullet points. Write in short paragraphs."
- **Tone and language:** "Reply in the language the customer uses. Avoid jargon."
- **Compliance:** "Do not give medical, legal or financial advice."
- **Disclaimers:** "End any answer about pricing with: [your disclaimer]."

## Directives or Training Context?

| | |
|---|---|
| **Messaging Directives** | *How* the AI communicates, whatever the topic. |
| **[Training Context](/pages/ai-config/training-context)** | *What* the AI knows: products, policies, answers. |

If something sounds odd, check for conflicts. A directive that says "be brief" and an assistant prompt that says "give detailed answers" will pull the AI two ways. Keep your directives short and consistent.
