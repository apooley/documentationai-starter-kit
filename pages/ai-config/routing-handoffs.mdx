Two things happen when a conversation needs to go somewhere else:

1. **Routing Rules** decide *when* a conversation moves to a different assistant.
2. **Handoff Settings** decide *how* that move happens: the message the customer sees, limits to prevent endless transfers, and your business hours.

Open **AI Assistant → Routing & Handoffs**. The two tabs are **Routing Rules** and **Handoff Settings**.

<Callout kind="info">
How it fits together: a rule matches → Handoff Settings check the safety limits → if allowed, the customer gets the handoff message and the conversation moves.
</Callout>

## Routing Rules

A routing rule says "if a conversation looks like *this*, send it to *that* assistant." For example: if a customer writes "pricing", pass them to your Sales assistant.

The rules table shows **Rule Name, Triggers, Target, Channels, Priority, Status** and **Actions** (edit or delete). Use **Search rules...** to find one and **Filter by Assistant** to show **All Rules**, **Organization-Level Rules**, or the rules for one assistant.

### Create a rule

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Create Rule">
    On the **Routing Rules** tab, click **Create Rule**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it">
    Enter a **Rule Name**, for example "Route sales inquiries to sales agent".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose where it applies">
    Under **Assistant**, pick **Organization-Level Rule** to apply across your whole account, or pick one assistant so the rule only applies while that assistant is handling the conversation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the trigger">
    - **Trigger Keywords (comma-separated)**: words or phrases such as `pricing, cost, buy, purchase`. Any one of them can trigger the rule. Extra spaces and repeated words are tidied up when you click out of the box.
    - **Trigger Intent (optional)**: let the AI recognise what the customer wants. Choose Sales, Support, Billing, Technical or General.

    You need at least one keyword or an intent. You can use both.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the Target Assistant">
    Pick the assistant that should take over. This is required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick channels">
    Tick any of **sms**, **chat**, **voice**, **email**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Priority and Status">
    **Priority** is a number; higher numbers are checked first. Use low priority for general rules and high priority for exceptions. Set **Status** to **Active** or **Inactive** (inactive rules are kept but ignored).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save Rule**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To change a rule later, use the edit icon in the Actions column. Deleting asks you to confirm and can't be undone.

<Callout kind="tip">
You can also reach an assistant's rules from **AI Assistant → Assistants**: each assistant card has a **Rules** button.
</Callout>

<Callout kind="info">
Rules move a conversation to another **assistant**. To bring in a person, use the escalation settings on the assistant (Escalation Triggers and Offer Handoff After) and your inbox. See [AI Assistants](/pages/ai-config/assistants).
</Callout>

## Handoff Settings

### Turn handoffs on

Switch on **Enable Agent Handoff**. This lets assistants transfer conversations to specialised assistants. When it's off, routing-based transfers and [warm transfer](/pages/ai-config/warm-transfer) between assistants don't happen, and the settings below are hidden.

### What the customer sees

- **Message Style**: Default, Professional, Casual, Brief or Detailed. Each fills in a ready-made message.
- **Handoff Message Template**: edit the message. You can use these placeholders, which are swapped for real details:
  - `{agent_name}`: the assistant taking over
  - `{task_description}`: what they specialise in
  - `{customer_intent}`: what the customer needs
- **Show Preview** / **Hide Preview**: see how it will read.
- **Human Response Time**: what the AI tells customers when a person will reply, such as "Within 1 business day".

### Guardrails

Guardrails stop customers being bounced around. If a guardrail blocks a transfer, the conversation stays where it is, even when a rule matches.

| Setting | What it does | Default |
|---|---|---|
| **Maximum Handoffs Per Conversation** | Most times one conversation can be transferred (1 to 10). | 3 |
| **Cooldown: Minimum Messages** | Messages that must pass after a handoff before another is allowed. | 2 |
| **Cooldown: Minimum Minutes** | Minutes that must pass after a handoff before another is allowed. Both cooldowns must be satisfied. | 5 |
| **Prevent Handoff Loops** | Stops a conversation going back to an assistant that already handled it (A → B → A). | On |
| **Maximum Handoffs Per Hour (Optional)** | Caps handoffs per hour. Leave empty for no limit. | No limit |

### Business Hours Routing

Tell Autoch.at when you're open so routing behaves sensibly after hours.

- **Timezone**: for example `America/New_York`.
- **Weekly Schedule**: tick **Open** for each day and set the start and end times (one block per day).
- **Holiday Overrides**: click **Add Holiday**, pick a date, and tick **Closed** if you're closed that day. Use **Remove** to delete one.
- **After-Hours Behavior**: what happens to routing outside your hours: **Queue (skip routing rules)**, **Defer**, **Auto Reply**, or **Allow routing**.

Click **Save Settings** to save the whole tab.

## Tips

- Start with a few clear rules. Overlapping rules with similar keywords confuse results.
- Test with the [Widget Playground](/pages/conversations/widget-playground) before relying on a new rule.
- If a transfer doesn't happen, check Enable Agent Handoff is on, then check the guardrails and your business hours.
