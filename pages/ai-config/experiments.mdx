An **experiment** splits real conversations between two or more [playbooks](/pages/ai-config/playbooks) so you can see which one works better, instead of guessing.

Open **AI Assistant → Experiments** in the sidebar.

<Callout kind="info">
Experiments is an advanced tool. It appears only for admins and for team members who have been given Experiments permission. If you don't see it, ask an admin. Experiments that apply to the whole account (shown as **Tenant** in the Scope column) can only be changed by an admin.
</Callout>

## When to run one

Experiments are worth it when a change could really affect results, for example:

- A different opening message in a lead-qualification playbook.
- A direct tone versus a conversational one.
- Offering a demo early versus late in the conversation.

For small fixes such as typos, just edit and publish the playbook.

## The Experiments page

- **Workspace**: experiments belong to the workspace you've selected in the sidebar.
- **Status** filter: All statuses, Draft, Active, Paused, Completed or Archived. Tick **Include archived** to see old ones. Click **Refresh** to reload.
- **Experiment List**: Name, Status, Scope (a workspace name, or Tenant for account-wide), and Updated.
- Click any row to open its details on the right.

## Set up an experiment

You need two or more playbooks first. Duplicate your current playbook and change the copy (see [AI Playbooks](/pages/ai-config/playbooks)).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Fill in Create Experiment">
    Enter an **Experiment name** and a **Description**. Optionally set a start and end date and time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the variants">
    Under **Initial Variants**, each row is one playbook being tested. Give it a **Name**, the playbook's ID in the **playbook_id** box, and a **Weight**. Tick **Control** on the row that is your current playbook.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the draft">
    Click **Create Draft Experiment**. It appears in the list with status Draft. Nothing is live yet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Adjust if needed">
    Select the experiment to edit its name, description and dates and click **Save Experiment**. Under **Variants** you can edit a row and click **Save**, or use **Add Variant** to add another.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">
**Weight** controls how much of the traffic each variant gets. Equal weights give an even split. Give the untested playbook a smaller weight if you want to limit risk.
</Callout>

## Check it, then start it

In **Preflight & Lifecycle**:

1. Click **Preflight Activate** (or **Preflight Schedule** if you've picked dates). A report says **Eligible** or **Blocked**, with a list of **Errors** (must fix) and **Warnings** (worth reading).
2. When it's eligible, click **Activate** to start now, or set **Schedule start** / **Schedule end** and click **Schedule**.
3. You're asked to confirm.

While it runs you can use:

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Pause** | Pauses the experiment (status becomes Paused). |
| **Complete** | Marks the experiment as Completed. |
| **Stop** | Stops the experiment. |
| **Archive** | Moves it to Archived. It's hidden unless you tick **Include archived**. |

### Roll out gradually

The **Rollout** section shows the rollout state, the **Percentage** of traffic, and any hold reason. Admins can click **Apply default ramp plan**, **Hold** (with a **Hold reason**), **Resume**, or **Promote cohort** (with a **Target %**) after ticking the checks **Preflight OK**, **Baseline OK** and **Guardrails OK**. **Refresh monitoring** shows the next check time and current cohort.

## Read the results

**Health Snapshot (Last 30 Days)** lists each experiment with a health status, how many conversations were **Exposures**, how many **Converted**, and the **Conversion** percentage.

<Callout kind="info">
Give an experiment plenty of conversations before you decide. A small sample can mislead you.
</Callout>

## Pick a winner

In **Winner & Sunset**:

1. Choose the best variant in **Select winner variant**. Add a **Reason (optional)**.
2. Click **Promote winner**. Confirm "Promote this variant to default playbook behavior?"
3. Click **Sunset losers** to retire the others.
4. Changed your mind? Paste the promotion's ID into **Promotion audit log id (rollback)** and click **Rollback winner**.

## Clean up and history

- **Audit Timeline** lists every action taken on the experiment, who did it and when. Click **Load More** for older entries.
- **Retention** shows how much old experiment data can be removed. **Dry-run cleanup** previews it. **Run cleanup** permanently deletes it and can't be undone.
