---
title: AI Playbooks
description: Playbooks tell an AI assistant what to achieve and how to behave, with versions you can publish or roll back. Learn to create, edit, assign and test them.
---

An assistant's System Prompt says *who* it is. A **playbook** says *what it is trying to achieve* and what rules to follow: its goals, tone, limits, safety rules and channel behavior. Playbooks are versioned, so you can change them safely and go back if a change doesn't work.

Open **AI Assistant → AI Playbooks** in the sidebar.

<Callout kind="info">
AI Playbooks only appears in the sidebar if it is turned on for your account. Only admins can link playbooks to assistants.
</Callout>

## The Playbooks page

- The **left list** shows your playbooks with their status, version number and last-updated date.
- Click one to open it on the right. The header shows **Save Draft** and **Publish New Version**, plus **Open Playground** to test it.
- The **Used by:** line shows which assistants follow this playbook.
- Ten tabs hold the settings: **Identity, Tone, Goals, Constraints, Safety, Channels, Tools, Model, Context, Versions**.

## Create a playbook

### Option 1: Create via Chat (recommended)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Create via Chat">
    On the Playbooks page, click **Create via Chat**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Answer the questions">
    The builder interviews you about your business and works through Identity, Tone, Goals, Constraints, Safety, Channels and Tools. A progress bar shows where you are.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the draft">
    Use **Draft Preview** to read what has been captured, and **Test Draft** to chat with it before saving.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name and publish">
    Under **Finalize**, enter a **Playbook Name** (just for your own reference). Leave **Activate this playbook immediately** ticked if you want it live, then click **Publish Playbook**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Option 2: Start from a template

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click New Playbook">
    In the **Create Playbook** window enter a **Name**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a Template">
    Choose **General Assistant** (balanced default), **Lead Qualification** (qualify inbound leads and collect contact details) or **Customer Support** (troubleshoot, then hand off to a person when needed). A short description appears under the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Create">
    The playbook opens so you can adjust each tab.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What each tab does

| Tab | What you set |
|---|---|
| **Identity** | **Base Prompt**, **Persona** and **Display Name** (the name the assistant introduces itself with). |
| **Tone** | **Formality** (Casual / Professional / Formal), **Empathy**, **Verbosity** (Concise / Balanced / Detailed) and **Custom Tone Rules** (one per line). |
| **Goals** | **Primary Goal**, **Secondary Goals**, **Success Criteria** (one per line) and the switch **Close chat after goals met**. |
| **Constraints** | **Never Mention**, **Never Do** and **Must Include**. |
| **Safety** | **Escalation Triggers** and **Escalation Threshold**, **Profanity Handling** (Ignore / Acknowledge / Escalate), **PII Handling** (Collect / Redact / Refuse) and **Emergency Protocol**. |
| **Channels** | Turn each channel on or off and add channel-specific prompt additions, tone and maximum reply length (use **Inherit** to keep the main setting). |
| **Tools** | **Allowed Tools**, **Denied Tools** and **Require Approval**. Advanced; most businesses leave this alone. |
| **Model** | **Provider**, **Model ID** and **Temperature**. Advanced. |
| **Context** | **Document IDs** and **Custom Instructions** that apply whenever this playbook is used. |
| **Versions** | History of every version. |

<Callout kind="tip">
Goals work best written as a short list of what a good outcome looks like, for example "Collect name, company and email" and "Offer to book a demo". Turn on **Close chat after goals met** for one-shot flows such as booking or lead capture, and leave it off for support, where customers may have follow-up questions.
</Callout>

## Save, publish and roll back

1. Make your changes and click **Save Draft** to keep them without affecting live conversations.
2. When you're happy, click **Publish New Version**. It becomes the active version.
3. Open the **Versions** tab to see all versions. For each you can:
   - **View**: see its details and a "Diff vs Active" comparison.
   - **Load into Editor**: copy it into the editor (this overwrites what you're currently editing, so you're asked to confirm).
   - **Activate**: make that version live. Use this to roll back.

## Use a playbook with an assistant

An assistant follows **one playbook at a time**. There are two ways to connect them (admins only):

**From the Playbooks page**

1. Open the playbook and click **Assign to assistants**.
2. Tick the assistants that should use it. If an assistant already uses a different playbook, a note says it will be switched.
3. Click **Save assignments**. Unticking an assistant removes the playbook from it.

**From the assistant**

1. Go to **AI Assistant → Assistants** and click **Edit** on the assistant.
2. Choose a playbook in the **Playbook** list (or **No playbook**) and save.

See [AI Assistants](/pages/ai-config/assistants).

## Duplicate or archive

Click the **⋮** (Playbook actions) menu on a playbook in the list:

- **Duplicate** makes a copy you can change without touching the original. Great for [Experiments](/pages/ai-config/experiments).
- **Archive** removes it from use. To bring an archived playbook back, contact support.

## Test a playbook

Click **Open Playground**, pick an assistant that uses the playbook, and chat as a customer would. See [Widget Playground](/pages/conversations/widget-playground).
