AI assistants talk to real customers on your behalf. **AI Safety** gives you two simple lists to keep them on-brand and careful:

- **Blocked Keywords**: words or phrases the AI must never use in a reply.
- **Serious Mode Keywords**: words in a customer's message that tell the AI the topic is serious, so it answers in a more professional, empathetic and careful way.

Open **AI Assistant → AI Safety** in the sidebar.

<Callout kind="info">
Only organization **admins** can open this page. Other team members see "Access Denied."
</Callout>

## How the safety net works

Autoch.at combines two sets of keywords:

1. **System boundaries**: built-in keywords that are always on. You can see them under each box, but you can't remove them.
2. **Your keywords**: the ones you add.

When they combine, the stricter rule wins. If the system blocks something, it stays blocked even if it isn't in your list. Under each box, **Effective (combined)** shows the full list actually being used.

## Blocked Keywords

Use this for words or phrases your AI should never say, such as a competitor's name, a confidential project name, or a term you don't want linked to your brand. If the AI tries to use one in a reply, that reply is blocked automatically.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open AI Safety">
    Go to **AI Assistant → AI Safety**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your words">
    In **Blocked Keywords**, type one keyword or phrase per line.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save Settings** at the bottom. You'll see "AI safety keywords saved successfully."
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Serious Mode Keywords

Some conversations need extra care: legal threats, fraud, safety concerns, formal complaints. In Serious Mode the AI becomes more professional, empathetic and careful.

Autoch.at already watches for a built-in set of serious topics. You can add your own in two lists:

| List | What happens | Example words |
|---|---|---|
| **High Confidence Keywords** | Triggers Serious Mode immediately. | lawsuit, legal action, fraud, theft |
| **Medium Confidence Keywords** | Autoch.at double-checks with AI before switching. | very upset, extremely frustrated, privacy violation |

Type one keyword or phrase per line, then click **Save Settings**.

<Callout kind="tip">
Start small. A long list of common words can switch the AI into Serious Mode too often. Check real conversations in [Conversations](/pages/conversations/conversations) to see if the tone is right, then adjust your lists.
</Callout>

## If the page won't load

You'll see "Couldn't load your safety settings." Your saved keywords haven't changed. Click **Retry**. Autoch.at will not let you save while the page failed to load, so you can't accidentally wipe your lists.
