# Features

Autoch.at brings your customer conversations, AI assistants, CRM and social media into one place. Some features depend on your plan. If you do not see something described here, check **Usage & Billing** or ask [support@autoch.at](mailto:support@autoch.at).

## AI assistants

- **[Assistants](/pages/ai-config/assistants)**: create several assistants, each with its own name, personality, channels and knowledge. Clone an assistant to try a variation.
- **[Playbooks](/pages/ai-config/playbooks)**: step-by-step conversation plans for goals such as qualifying a lead or booking an appointment. Each assistant can use its own playbook, and you can duplicate a playbook to test a variation. Only account administrators can change which playbook an assistant uses.
- **Warm transfer between assistants**: in web chat, an assistant can hand a visitor to a colleague assistant that suits the question better. The visitor sees "Connecting you with...", and the new assistant receives a short briefing so the visitor does not have to repeat themselves. You choose which colleagues each assistant may hand over to, and it is off by default.
- **[Training Context](/pages/ai-config/training-context)**: teach your assistants with your website, documents, FAQs and your product feed.
- **[Messaging Directives](/pages/ai-config/messaging-directives)**: workspace-wide rules for tone, length and wording.
- **[Routing & Handoffs](/pages/ai-config/routing-handoffs)**: decide which assistant or person handles a conversation, and when the AI should hand over.
- **[AI Safety](/pages/ai-config/ai-safety)**: blocked keywords and a Serious Mode for sensitive moments.
- **[Experiments](/pages/ai-config/experiments)**: compare two playbooks on real conversations.
- **[Widget Playground](/pages/conversations/widget-playground)**: try your assistant in a safe test area before it goes live.

## Conversations

- **One inbox** for website chat, [SMS](/pages/conversations/sms-workflows), [email](/pages/conversations/email-workflows), [voice calls](/pages/conversations/voice), and Facebook and Instagram messages.
- **[Conversation detail](/pages/conversations/conversation-detail)**: full history, notes, assignment, and the client record beside it.
- **Workflows and outbound**: automated [email workflows](/pages/conversations/email-workflows), [SMS workflows](/pages/conversations/sms-workflows) and [outbound email](/pages/conversations/outbound-email).
- **[Conversation tags](/pages/settings/conversation-tags)** to keep your team consistent and organised.
- **Booking**: connect Calendly so the assistant can offer appointment times. See [Calendar](/pages/settings/calendar).

## CRM

- **Built-in client records**: every person who contacts you is saved with their conversation history. See [Clients](/pages/crm/clients).
- **Quotes, invoices and projects**: the Autoch.at CRM also includes quotes, invoices, products, projects, time tracking and a customer portal.
- **Outside CRMs**: connect Microsoft Dynamics today. See [CRM Connections](/pages/crm/crm-connections) and [Integrations](/integrations).
- **[Qualification Settings](/pages/crm/qualification-settings)**: define what makes a good lead and test your rules.

## Insights

- **[Analytics](/pages/insights/analytics)**: conversation volume, qualified leads and channel health.
- **[Conversation Intelligence](/pages/insights/conversation-intelligence)**: what customers ask about, and how conversations end.

## Social media

- **[Profiles](/pages/social/social-profiles)** and accounts: connect your accounts. Facebook Messenger and Instagram messages arrive in your inbox. Other networks are used for publishing.
- **[Posts](/pages/social/social-posts)** and **[Queues](/pages/social/social-queues)**: schedule posts and set up regular posting times.
- **[Analytics](/pages/social/social-analytics)** and the **[Caption Generator](/pages/social/caption-generator)**.

## Settings and security

- **[Users](/pages/settings/users)**, **[Workspaces](/pages/settings/workspaces)**, **[Branding](/pages/settings/branding)** and the **[Chat Widget](/pages/settings/chat-widget)**, including proactive messages and tracking.
- **[Email](/pages/settings/email)**, **[Phone Numbers](/pages/settings/phone-numbers)** and **[Calendar](/pages/settings/calendar)**.
- **[PII & Privacy](/pages/settings/pii-privacy)**: control what personal information is stored or hidden.
- **[Bring your own keys](/pages/settings/byok)**: use your own OpenAI key (and a Vapi key for voice) on plans that include it.
- **[Usage & Billing](/pages/settings/billing)**: subscription and invoices, handled through Stripe.
- **Account security**: you must confirm your email before signing in, changing an email address requires confirming the new one (and your password), and signing out ends your session on every device.

## For developers

- **[Webhooks](/api-reference/webhooks)** send signed messages to your own tools when leads are qualified, appointments are booked and more.
- **[CRM REST API](/api-reference/crm-api)** with scoped API keys.
