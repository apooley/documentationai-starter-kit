# Welcome to Autoch.at

Autoch.at is one place to talk to your customers, wherever they reach you. AI assistants answer questions and capture leads around the clock, your team steps in when it matters, and everything lands in a built-in CRM so nothing gets lost.

## What is Autoch.at?

Your customers might message you on your website, by text, by email, by phone, or on Facebook and Instagram. Autoch.at brings all of those conversations into a single inbox. An AI assistant that has been taught about your business can answer the common questions, ask the right questions to qualify a lead, book appointments, and pass the conversation to a person when it should. When your team takes over, they see everything that was already said.

Every person who contacts you is saved as a client record with their conversation history. If you already use another CRM, you can connect it so the two stay in step.
