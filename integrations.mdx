# Integrations

This page lists what Autoch.at connects to today. Each connection is set up inside the app, usually in **Settings** or **CRM**.

<Callout kind="info">
Some integrations depend on your plan. If a setting does not appear for you, contact [support@autoch.at](mailto:support@autoch.at).
</Callout>

## Customer channels

| Channel | How to connect |
|---|---|
| **Website chat** | Paste the snippet from **Settings > Chat Widget** into your site. See [Chat Widget](/pages/settings/chat-widget). |
| **Email** | Connect your domain in **Settings > Email** and publish the DNS records shown. You can also create sending (SMTP) credentials, read an existing mailbox over IMAP, or connect a Gmail or Outlook mailbox. See [Email](/pages/settings/email). |
| **SMS and voice calls** | Get a phone number in **Settings > Phone Numbers**. Numbers are provided by Telnyx. See [Phone Numbers](/pages/settings/phone-numbers). |
| **Facebook Messenger and Instagram messages** | Open **Social Media > Accounts**, choose **Connect Meta Account** and sign in with Facebook. Messages then arrive in your inbox. See [Social Profiles](/pages/social/social-profiles). |

## Social publishing

Posts, queues and analytics use a connected social account. Supported networks include Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, X (Twitter), TikTok, YouTube, Pinterest, Reddit, Bluesky, Threads and Google Business Profile. Direct messages are only available for Facebook Messenger and Instagram. See [Social Posts](/pages/social/social-posts).

## CRM

Autoch.at has its own [built-in CRM](/pages/crm/clients), so you do not need another one. If you already use a CRM, you can connect it so records stay in sync.

| CRM | Status |
|---|---|
| **Microsoft Dynamics** | Available. Connect it in **CRM > CRM Connection** with a Microsoft sign-in, then choose how fields map between the two systems. |
| **Jobber** | Available to some accounts. Connect it from the same CRM Connection page. |
| **HubSpot** | Not available yet. It is planned for later and has no date. |
| **Salesforce** | Not available yet. It is planned for later and has no date. |

Need a CRM that is not listed? You can send data out with [Webhooks](/api-reference/webhooks), or use the [CRM REST API](/api-reference/crm-api).

See [CRM Connections](/pages/crm/crm-connections) for setup steps.

## Calendar and booking

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Calendly** | Connect Calendly in **Settings > Calendar** so your assistant can offer appointment times during a conversation. You paste in a Personal Access Token from your Calendly account. |

You can also send booking results back from your own booking system with the appointment callback in [Webhooks](/api-reference/webhooks).

## AI models

Autoch.at provides the AI for you. On plans that include **BYOK** (bring your own keys), you can add your own **OpenAI** key to pay OpenAI directly, and a **Vapi** key for voice calls. See [BYOK](/pages/settings/byok).

## Billing

Subscriptions and invoices are handled by **Stripe**. See [Usage & Billing](/pages/settings/billing).

## Webhooks and automation

- **[Webhooks](/api-reference/webhooks)** send signed messages to tools such as n8n or your own server when something happens (for example, a lead is qualified).
- The **[CRM REST API](/api-reference/crm-api)** lets your software read and write clients, quotes, invoices, products and projects.
- Zapier and Make: you can connect them through webhooks and the CRM API. There is no Autoch.at app in their directories.
