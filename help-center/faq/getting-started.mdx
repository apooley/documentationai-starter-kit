# Getting Started FAQ

## Signing up

**What happens when I sign up?**
You fill in your name, company, email and password, choose a plan and any add-ons, review, and then finish on a secure Stripe page. After that, we email you a link to confirm your address. See the [Quickstart](/quickstart).

**Why can I not sign in yet?**
You have to confirm your email first. Open the email we sent and click the link. If it has not arrived, check spam or junk, then open the confirmation page and choose **Resend Verification Email**.

**Why did you ask for my phone number?**
Sometimes sign-up includes a quick check of your mobile number. We send a code by text message or voice call, and you type it in to continue.

**Is there a free trial?**
Yes. New accounts start on a trial, and **Usage & Billing** shows how many days are left and lets you subscribe. See [Usage & Billing](/pages/settings/billing).

## Setting up

**Do I need technical skills?**
No. You will paste one snippet into your website at the end, and your website builder or developer can tell you where. Everything else happens in the app.

**What should I do first after signing in?**
Follow the **Setup Wizard** that opens automatically. It takes you through creating your first assistant, adding business knowledge and testing. You can skip steps and come back later with **Resume Setup Wizard** or from **Settings > Preferences**.

**How do I teach the AI about my business?**
Open **Training Context** and enter your website address so the assistant can read it, then add your most common questions and any documents (price lists, policies). See [Training Context](/pages/ai-config/training-context).

**How do I install the chat widget on my website?**
1. Go to **Settings > Chat Widget**.
2. Add your website under **Allowed Domains**.
3. Click **Generate token**, then copy the snippet.
4. Paste it into your site just before the closing `</body>` tag.
5. Visit your site and send a test message.

If your website is built on WordPress or a site builder, use its option for adding custom code to the footer.

**Can I test the AI before putting it on my website?**
Yes. Open **Widget Playground** in the sidebar. It works like your real chat widget but only you can see it.

**Do I need a playbook?**
No. A playbook is optional and helps when you want the assistant to follow a plan, such as qualifying a lead. You can attach one when you create an assistant, or later. Playbooks need a plan that includes them. See [Playbooks](/pages/ai-config/playbooks).

## Your team

**How do I invite my team?**
Account administrators can invite people from **Settings > Users**. Enter their email and choose their role. They get an email with a link to set up their account.

**Why do I not see some menu items?**
Some items only show for administrators, and some depend on your plan (for example SMS, email, voice, playbooks and social media).
