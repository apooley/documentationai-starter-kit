# Troubleshooting common issues

Find your problem below. If none of these fix it, email [support@autoch.at](mailto:support@autoch.at) and tell us what you tried.

## I cannot sign in

**Symptoms:** You see a message about verifying your email, or sign-in is refused.

1. **Confirm your email.** You cannot sign in until you click the link in the confirmation email. Check spam or junk, then open the confirmation page and choose **Resend Verification Email**.
2. **If you recently changed your email address,** the new address must be confirmed too. Look for the confirmation email at the new address.
3. **Forgot your password?** Use **Forgot password** on the sign-in page.
4. **Invited by a teammate?** Use the link in your invitation email to set up your account first.

## Chat widget not appearing on my website

**Symptoms:** The chat bubble does not show on your site.

1. **Check the snippet.** It must be on the page just before the closing `</body>` tag. If you use a tag manager, make sure the tag runs on every page.
2. **Check Allowed Domains.** In **Settings > Chat Widget**, your exact website address must be listed under **Allowed Domains**. A missing `www.` version is a common cause.
3. **Check for blocking.** If your website restricts which outside scripts can load (a Content Security Policy), ask your web developer to allow Autoch.at.
4. **Clear your cache.** Hard refresh the page or use a private window.

## The AI started a reply but nothing appeared

The chat widget shows a **retry** option if a reply is interrupted. Click it to ask again. If this happens often, tell support the time it happened.

## Conversations not appearing in my inbox

**Symptoms:** You know messages were sent, but they are not in your inbox.

1. **Check the channel is set up.** The Dashboard's System Status shows which channels are ready.
2. **Check your filters.** Make sure the inbox is not filtered to a single channel or status.
3. **Check your workspace.** The workspace selector in the sidebar controls what each page shows. Switch to the right one.
4. **Missing a menu item?** SMS, email, voice, playbooks and social media depend on your plan. If an item is missing, check **Usage & Billing** or contact support.
5. **For email,** check that your domain shows as verified in **Settings > Email** and the DNS records are published exactly as shown.

## AI assistant giving wrong answers

1. **Add more Training Context.** Add the missing facts, especially FAQs for questions it gets wrong. See [Training Context](/pages/ai-config/training-context).
2. **Look for conflicts.** If two sources disagree, fix or remove one.
3. **Adjust the assistant.** Update its instructions on the [Assistants](/pages/ai-config/assistants) page. Be specific about tone and what it should not do.
4. **Use Messaging Directives** for rules that apply to every assistant, such as never quoting prices. See [Messaging Directives](/pages/ai-config/messaging-directives).
5. **Test your change** in the [Widget Playground](/pages/conversations/widget-playground).

## AI not handing over to a person when it should

1. **Review your handoff rules** in [Routing & Handoffs](/pages/ai-config/routing-handoffs).
2. **Add a clear instruction** to the assistant, for example: "If a customer is upset, asks for a person, or has a billing dispute, hand the conversation to the team."
3. **Check your notifications** in **Settings > Notifications** so the right people are told.

## Connecting an email domain does not work

1. In **Settings > Email**, copy each DNS record exactly into your domain provider.
2. Allow time for the changes to take effect, then verify again.
3. If emails go to spam, make sure the SPF and DKIM records are in place.

## Social account connection failing

1. **Use the right login.** Sign in with an account that manages the Page or profile.
2. **Accept every permission** during connection. Declining some can make the connection fail or work only in part.
3. **Reconnect.** Go to **Social Media > Accounts**, remove the account and connect it again.
4. **Still failing?** Email support with the message you see.
