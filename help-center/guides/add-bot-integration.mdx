# Connecting your first channel

A channel is a way customers reach you. Once a channel is connected, its conversations arrive in your inbox and your AI assistant can answer them. Start with website chat, which is the quickest.

<Callout kind="info">
Email, SMS and voice depend on your plan. If you do not see **Email** or **Phone Numbers** in Settings, your plan may not include them. Check **Usage & Billing** or contact [support@autoch.at](mailto:support@autoch.at).
</Callout>

After each channel, check the **System Status** panel on your [Dashboard](/pages/overview/dashboard). It shows what is ready.

## Website chat

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Chat Widget settings">
    Go to **Settings > Chat Widget**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your website">
    Under **Allowed Domains**, add the address of the website where the chat should appear.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate and copy the snippet">
    Click **Generate token**, then copy the snippet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste it on your website">
    Paste the snippet into your website just before the closing `</body>` tag. On website builders and WordPress, use the option for adding custom code to the footer, or ask your web developer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test it">
    Try the [Widget Playground](/pages/conversations/widget-playground) first, then visit your website and send a message. It appears under **Conversations**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can change how the chat looks in [Branding](/pages/settings/branding) and [Chat Widget](/pages/settings/chat-widget).

## Email

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Email settings">
    Go to **Settings > Email**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your domain">
    Enter your website's domain, for example `yourcompany.com` (not a full email address).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish the DNS records">
    Autoch.at shows records to add where your domain is managed (usually your domain registrar or website host). Copy them exactly. If you are unsure, send the list to whoever looks after your website.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify">
    Verify the domain once the records are published. This can take some time to take effect.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up sending">
    After the domain is verified, create the sending (SMTP) credentials that Autoch.at uses to send mail from it. You can also connect an existing mailbox. See [Email](/pages/settings/email).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## SMS and phone calls

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get a phone number">
    Go to **Settings > Phone Numbers** and add a number. The number is used for texts and, on plans with voice, for phone calls.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose which assistant answers">
    In your assistant's setup, under **Channels & Routing**, switch on SMS and/or voice. See [Assistants](/pages/ai-config/assistants).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test it">
    Send a text to the number, or call it, and check that the conversation appears under **SMS Conversations** or **Phone / Voice**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

More detail: [Phone Numbers](/pages/settings/phone-numbers), [SMS Workflows](/pages/conversations/sms-workflows) and [Voice Conversations](/pages/conversations/voice).

## Facebook Messenger and Instagram messages

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Social Media > Accounts">
    Choose **Connect Meta Account**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in with Facebook">
    Sign in with an account that manages the Facebook Page or Instagram professional account, and accept the permissions requested.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Once connected, new messages from that Page or account arrive in your inbox.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Other social networks (such as LinkedIn and X) can be connected for scheduling and publishing posts, but their private messages do not come into the inbox. See [Social Profiles](/pages/social/social-profiles).
