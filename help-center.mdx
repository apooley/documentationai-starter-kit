## Contact us

If you cannot find your answer, email [support@autoch.at](mailto:support@autoch.at). Tell us what you were trying to do, what you expected, what happened instead, and (if you can) paste any message you saw. A screenshot helps a lot.

You can also search this site using the search bar at the top of the page.

## Frequently asked questions

### Getting started

**Do I need technical skills?**
No. Setting up an assistant, teaching it about your business and adding the chat widget needs no coding. The widget is one snippet that you paste into your website. See the [Quickstart](/quickstart).

**Can I try Autoch.at before paying?**
New accounts start with a free trial. While you are on the trial, **Usage & Billing** shows how many trial days are left. You can also test your assistant at any time in the [Widget Playground](/pages/conversations/widget-playground) before it appears on your website.

**I did not get my confirmation email.**
Check your spam or junk folder, then open the confirmation page and choose **Resend Verification Email**. You must confirm your email before you can sign in.

### AI assistants

**Why is my assistant giving wrong answers?**
Usually it does not have the information yet. Add more to [Training Context](/pages/ai-config/training-context), especially FAQs for the exact questions it gets wrong. If two sources disagree, remove or fix one.

**How do I stop the AI talking about certain topics?**
Use [AI Safety](/pages/ai-config/ai-safety) to block words or topics, and [Messaging Directives](/pages/ai-config/messaging-directives) for rules that apply to every assistant.

**What is the difference between a playbook and training context?**
Training Context is what your assistant *knows* about your business. A [playbook](/pages/ai-config/playbooks) is the *plan* it follows to reach a goal, such as qualifying a lead or booking a meeting. Each assistant can have its own playbook.

**Can one assistant pass a customer to another?**
In web chat, yes, if you turn on transfers for that assistant and choose which colleagues it may hand over to. See [Assistants](/pages/ai-config/assistants).

### Conversations

**Why are conversations not showing up in my inbox?**
Check that the channel is set up (Dashboard > System Status shows this), then check the filter at the top of the inbox and the workspace selected in the sidebar. See [Troubleshooting](/help-center/troubleshooting/missing-documents).

**How does a person take over from the AI?**
Set up when the AI should hand over under [Routing & Handoffs](/pages/ai-config/routing-handoffs). Handed-over conversations show in your inbox with the full history, and you can assign a conversation to a team member. See [Conversation Detail](/pages/conversations/conversation-detail).

### Account and billing

**Where do I see my plan and invoices?**
In [Usage & Billing](/pages/settings/billing). Payments are handled by Stripe, and the billing portal there lets you update your card and see invoices.

**I changed my email address and cannot sign in.**
When an email address is changed, the new address must be confirmed first. Look for the confirmation email at the new address. Changing your own email also asks for your current password.

**I signed out on one device. Am I signed out everywhere?**
Yes. Signing out ends your session on all devices.

## Troubleshooting

For longer step-by-step fixes, see [Troubleshooting common issues](/help-center/troubleshooting/missing-documents).

### Chat widget not appearing

1. Make sure the snippet is on the page, just before the closing `</body>` tag.
2. In **Settings > Chat Widget**, check your website address is listed under **Allowed Domains**.
3. If your site blocks outside scripts (a Content Security Policy), ask your web developer to allow Autoch.at.
4. Refresh the page with your browser cache cleared, or try a private window.

### Email not receiving messages

1. In **Settings > Email**, check your domain shows as verified.
2. Confirm the DNS records shown there are published exactly as given at your domain provider. Changes can take a while to spread.
3. Check your spam folder. A new sending domain can be filtered at first.

### AI assistant not responding

1. Check the assistant is not paused and is set up for the channel you are testing in **Assistants**.
2. Ask an administrator to check **AI Safety**. A blocked word can stop a reply.
3. Open the conversation in your inbox. The timeline shows what happened.
4. Check your plan includes the channel (for example SMS, email or voice).

### Social account connection failing

1. Sign in with an account that manages the Page or profile you are connecting.
2. Accept every permission requested during connection.
3. Disconnect the account and connect it again from **Social Media > Accounts**.
4. If it still fails, email support with the message you see.
