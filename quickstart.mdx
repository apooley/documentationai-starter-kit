# Quickstart

This guide takes you from a brand-new account to an AI assistant answering visitors on your website. You do not need any technical skills. You will paste one short snippet into your website at the end.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Go to the sign-up page and fill in the four steps:

    1. **Account Info**: your full name, company name, email address and a password. If asked, also enter your mobile number and the code we send you by text or voice call.
    2. **Select Plan**: choose the plan that fits. You can change it later in **Usage & Billing**.
    3. **Add-ons**: optional extras such as more workspaces, voice or social media.
    4. **Review**: check everything and accept the terms.

    You are then taken to a secure Stripe page to finish. Payments are handled by Stripe.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm your email">
    We send a confirmation email to the address you signed up with. Click the link inside it. **You cannot sign in until you do this.**

    <Callout kind="tip">
    No email after a few minutes? Check your spam or junk folder, then open the confirmation page and use **Resend Verification Email**.
    </Callout>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in and follow the Setup Wizard">
    The first time you sign in, the **Setup Wizard** opens by itself. It walks you through the essentials and you can **Skip for Now** on any step. Your progress is saved, so close it whenever you like and come back with **Resume Setup Wizard** on the Dashboard, or from **Settings > Preferences**.

    The wizard covers: Welcome, Explore your dashboard, Create your first assistant, Add business knowledge and Test your setup. The steps below explain each one in more detail.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your first assistant">
    Go to **Assistants** and create a new one. The setup flow asks for the basics (what it is called and how it should sound), the channels it should handle (start with Chat), the knowledge it should use and how it should behave, then a review.

    There is also an optional step called **Playbook (recommended)**. A playbook tells the assistant how to run a conversation toward a goal, such as qualifying a lead. If you already have one, pick it here. If not, you can add one later (see [Playbooks](/pages/ai-config/playbooks)). Playbooks need a plan that includes them, and only an account administrator can attach one to an assistant.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Teach it about your business">
    Go to **Training Context**. The quickest start is to enter your website address so the assistant can read your site, then add the questions customers ask most (FAQs) and upload documents such as price lists or policies. See [Training Context](/pages/ai-config/training-context).

    <Callout kind="tip">
    Do not try to add everything at once. Start with your website and your top FAQs, then add more as you see what customers ask.
    </Callout>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the chat widget to your website">
    1. Go to **Settings > Chat Widget**.
    2. Under **Allowed Domains**, add your website address.
    3. Click **Generate token**, then copy the code snippet.
    4. Paste the snippet into your website just before the closing `</body>` tag. On WordPress you can use a plugin that adds code to the footer. Your web developer or website builder's "custom code" setting will know where it goes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test it in the Widget Playground">
    Go to **Widget Playground**. Chat with your assistant the way a customer would. Ask the usual questions, try something it should not know, and try to make it go off-topic. Improve your Training Context and instructions based on what you see, then visit your own website and send a test message through the real widget.
  </Step>
</Steps>

That is it. Your assistant is live, and every conversation appears in your inbox under **Conversations**.

## What to do next

- **Add more channels**: connect [email](/pages/settings/email), [SMS and phone numbers](/pages/settings/phone-numbers), or [Facebook and Instagram](/pages/social/social-profiles).
- **Decide when people take over**: set up [Routing & Handoffs](/pages/ai-config/routing-handoffs).
- **Invite your team** from **Settings > Users** (administrators only).
- **Connect a CRM**: your clients are saved automatically in the built-in CRM. See [CRM Connections](/pages/crm/crm-connections) if you use another one.
- **Check how it is going** after a few days in [Analytics](/pages/insights/analytics).

Questions? See the [Getting Started FAQ](/help-center/faq/getting-started) or the [Help Center](/help-center).
