# Changelog

What is new in Autoch.at, newest first. Autoch.at is a hosted service, so every update is applied for you automatically. There is nothing to install or upgrade.

## October 2026

<Update label="2026-10-14" description="Playbooks per assistant and warm transfer" tags={["feature"]} id="2026-10-14">

### New

- **A playbook for each assistant.** Choose which playbook an assistant uses from the assistant editor. When you create an assistant, there is now an optional **Playbook (recommended)** step. The Playbooks page shows which assistants use each playbook and lets you assign them. Only account administrators can change an assistant's playbook; everyone else sees it read-only. See [Playbooks](/pages/ai-config/playbooks).
- **Warm transfer between assistants (web chat).** An assistant can hand a visitor to a colleague assistant that is a better fit. The visitor sees "Connecting you with...", and the new assistant gets a short briefing so nobody has to repeat themselves. You choose which colleagues each assistant may hand over to. It is off by default. See [Assistants](/pages/ai-config/assistants).

</Update>

<Update label="2026-10-13" description="Account security, assistants and CRM fixes" tags={["security", "improvement", "fix"]} id="2026-10-13">

### Security

- **Changing an email address now needs confirmation.** When an email address is changed, the person is signed out until they confirm the new address by clicking a link we send there. Changing your own email also asks for your current password.
- **Sign out really signs you out.** Signing out, changing or resetting a password, changing an email, or turning off multi-factor sign-in now ends your session on every device.
- **Signing in to the CRM from Autoch.at needs a confirmed email address.**
- **Invitation links stay private.** Invitation and verification links are no longer visible to administrators in the team list.

### Fixes and improvements

- New accounts can now sign in right after confirming their email. Previously some owners could not.
- Assistants: errors from cloning, deleting or setting a default assistant now appear right where you clicked. Errors in the assistant editor show inside the editor window. The internal name is now optional.
- Qualification Settings: test results are written in plain English.
- Routing: keyword fields now accept commas as you type.
- Conversations: refreshing the page keeps your place, and links that choose a channel (for example `?channel=sms`) now open on that channel.
- CRM customer portal: invoice PDFs download correctly and the booking link works. Customers' first sign-in and profile saving are fixed.
- CRM quotes and invoices: quotes can no longer create duplicate invoices, and a quote's expiry date now counts the whole day.

</Update>

<Update label="2026-10-12" description="Chat widget and shortcut fixes" tags={["improvement", "fix"]} id="2026-10-12">

### Fixes and improvements

- **Chat widget:** if a reply is interrupted or comes back empty, the widget now shows a **retry** option.
- **Keyboard shortcuts** no longer take over your browser's copy, select all, save and reload commands. Navigation shortcuts are now two keys: press **g** then a letter (for example **g c** for Conversations), and **?** shows the full list.
- **AI Safety:** the page will not let you save while your keyword list has failed to load.
- **Conversations:** searching text that contains special characters now highlights safely.
- **CRM quotes:** accepting a quote now asks you to confirm, and cancelling the prompt when rejecting a quote now stops the rejection.

</Update>

<Update label="2026-10-09" description="Assistant cards" tags={["improvement"]} id="2026-10-09">

- The action buttons on each assistant card are now arranged in a neat two-by-two grid so they fit on smaller screens.

</Update>

<Update label="2026-10-06" description="Assistant cloning, Meta messages, workspaces and tracking" tags={["feature", "improvement"]} id="2026-10-06">

### New features

- **Assistant cloning.** Clone an existing assistant into any workspace in your account, with the option to copy its playbook too.
- **Playbook duplication.** Duplicate a playbook to create a variation for testing.
- **Close chat after goals are met.** A new playbook option closes the conversation automatically once the playbook's goals are achieved.
- **Manual structured-email (ADF) resend.** From the conversation page you can resend the structured email to your sales system, rebuilt from the current conversation.
- **Structured email on qualification.** A new setting controls whether the structured email is sent automatically when a lead is qualified (on by default).
- **Smarter timing for structured emails.** The system waits for a quiet moment in the conversation after a lead is first qualified before summarising and sending, and holds the send until the lead has an email or phone number on file.
- **Facebook and Instagram messages.** Direct messages from Facebook and Instagram now arrive in your inbox through a native Meta connection, including sign-in, message history and the required compliance callbacks.
- **Workspace switcher.** One switcher in the sidebar now replaces the separate selectors on each page, and it remembers your choice.
- **Tracking settings.** A new **Tracking** tab in Chat Widget settings lets you turn on tracking and map events to outcomes.
- **Chat started event.** Webhooks can send `autochat.chat_started.v1` when a visitor sends their first real message in the widget. See [Webhooks](/api-reference/webhooks).
- **Product link checking.** The AI now only shares product links it has found in your product feed, and says nothing rather than guessing.

### Improvements

- **Product feed retries.** A failed product feed update is retried after 1, 3 and 8 hours, then daily. Administrators are emailed once per run of failures.
- **Microsoft Dynamics notes.** Long conversations that exceed Dynamics' 10,000-character note limit now keep the most recent messages.
- **Contact details are remembered.** Phone numbers and emails typed into the chat widget are saved even when personal-information hiding is on, so the AI does not ask again.
- **Tidier new accounts.** Leftover empty default profiles from first-time setup are removed automatically.

### Fixes

- Fixed structured-email send records that were not always saved, and timestamp differences in follow-up records.
- Fixed the workspace switcher menu appearing behind the sidebar.
- Fixed conversation summaries for leads qualified immediately or after a delay.

</Update>
